On Monday we launched [Zoonk 2.0](/2026/zoonk-2), which turns your goal into a plan for every day. Zoonk 2.1 is about the time you spend on that plan. Lessons are easier to follow, practice calls feel like talking to a real person, and you get a certificate when you finish a level.

## Lessons that are easy to follow

When you're learning a new subject, like physics or economics, it's hard to tell which part matters. You finish a paragraph and you're not sure what you were supposed to remember.

So subject lessons now work like a teacher at a board. The main point gets underlined, and new terms are highlighted the way you'd mark them in your notebook. The example sits in its own frame, like the power light on your charger in a lesson about LEDs, so the idea and the case that shows it don't blur together. Lessons are written the way you'd explain something out loud, and they show a picture only when it helps you see the idea.

You also always know where you are. Each lesson opens with a map of its ideas, a strip at the top tells you which one you're on, and the map comes back at the end with everything you covered.

When a screen still doesn't click, tap "I didn't get this" and your buddy explains it again in simpler words.

## Practice calls that feel like calling someone

Speaking is the part most people put off. It's hard to practice without someone to talk to, and the real conversation always happens at a bad moment: at immigration, at the doctor, in a job interview.

Practice calls in Zoonk now work like a phone call. Say you're calling a landlord about an apartment on Queen Street. Before the call, you see how it works and what you need to get across, and you call when you're ready. What you both say shows up as you talk, so you can read along, and a bar tells you whose turn it is. The landlord answers in about a second and a half, and when you're done, says goodbye like a person would. Then you see which goals you met and the phrases you said well.

If your connection drops, tap "Call again" before the call's time runs out and you pick up where you stopped. The character knows you got cut off, and your feedback covers the whole call.

Characters who know you also remember what you've told them. Mention that you're moving to Toronto, and the next time you talk to your neighbor, they might ask how the move is going. You can see everything Zoonk remembers in your settings and delete any of it.

These screens are from a sample plan for a Portuguese speaker learning English, so the call's title and descriptions are in Portuguese.

## A certificate for every level you finish

Finishing a level takes weeks of work, and your manager, your school or a recruiter should be able to see it.

With Plus, you now get a certificate for each course level you finish and for each language level you reach, from A1 to C1. It has your name, what you studied and when you finished. Share it as a picture, add it to your LinkedIn profile, or send its link. Anyone who opens the link can check that it's real.

## Also new

While Zoonk builds your plan or writes your first lesson, you can answer quick questions about your subject, like what happens in the two-slit experiment when you watch which slit each particle goes through. Getting them wrong is part of the point, since it helps you remember later.

Lessons you've finished now have their own page, Completed lessons, so you can go back and do one again. If you're studying for two exams that cover the same subjects, they share those lessons, so you study each topic once and it counts for both. And if your connection drops in the middle of a lesson, your answers wait on your device and are sent when you're back online.
