# Zoonk 2.1: clearer lessons, better calls and certificates

<PostMeta date="November 7, 2026" authorId="will" />

We launched [Zoonk 2.0](/2026/zoonk-2) on Monday, and since then most of our work has gone into what got in people's way. Zoonk 2.1 is out today.

## Lessons that are easier to follow

A beginner who tried an early version of our new lessons told us the explanations felt like Yoda wrote them. That was fair. Lessons now explain each idea in plain sentences, in the order you'd say them out loud, and they show a picture only when it helps you see something.

The lesson screen looks like a teacher's board now, with the main point underlined, new terms highlighted and the example in its own frame. A strip at the top tells you which idea you're on, and each lesson starts and ends with a map of its ideas. If a screen doesn't click, tap "I didn't get this" and Zu explains it more simply.

New beginner plans also start with the subject itself. A biology plan opens with cells, and the chemistry and math it needs come right before you use them.

## Calls that feel like calls

Practice calls now start when you're ready. An intro explains how the call works, and you tap to call the character. The transcript follows along, you can see whose turn it is, and the character starts talking in about a second and a half. When time's up, they say goodbye at a natural pause. If your connection drops, tap "Call again" and the call picks up where it stopped.

Characters who know you also remember what you've told them. If you mentioned your move to Toronto, a neighbor might ask how it's going. Strangers never act as if they know you, and you can see or delete what Zoonk remembers in your settings.

## Certificates for what you finish

With Plus, you get a certificate for each course level you finish and each language level you reach, from A1 to C1. You can share it, add it to LinkedIn or send someone its public page, where anyone can check that it's real.

On the free plan, you still earn certificates and see them locked. Subscribing later unlocks the ones you've already earned, and they stay yours if Plus ends.

## Smaller things

- While we build your plan, you can answer quick questions about your subject.
- Tap a finished lesson on Today to do it again, or find it on the new Completed lessons page.
- If you're studying for two exams that cover the same subjects, they share those lessons, so you only study them once.
- Answers you give while offline wait on your device and are sent when you're back online.
- The sign-in page appears right away, and course and lesson pages load faster outside the US.

The [release notes](https://github.com/zoonk/zoonk/releases/tag/v2.1) list everything else, including a long list of fixes.
