## 1. What we collect

- **Account:** your email, name, username, profile picture, country, and language.
- **Learning:** your goals, plans, lessons, answers (typed, or spoken and turned into text), progress, mistakes, time spent, feedback, and preferences such as your buddy.
- **Memory:** short facts about how you learn that Zoonk remembers to help you (see section 4).
- **What you send us:** goals you describe, chats and calls with your buddy (the AI tutor), uploaded files, pasted text, and support messages.
- **Payments:** your plan and subscription status. Card details stay with the payment provider.
- **Technical and usage data:** IP address, device, browser, cookies, access logs, security events, and how you use Zoonk (screens, clicks or taps, and session replays).

---

## 2. Why we use it

- To run Zoonk: your account, your plan, your lessons, and your progress.
- To personalize your learning: lessons, examples, tutor answers, and suggestions.
- To take payments, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce fair-use limits.
- To find bugs, check the quality of AI content, and improve Zoonk.
- To meet legal obligations.

We use your data to provide the service you asked for, for our legitimate interests in running and improving it, and to comply with the law. We don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

---

## 3. AI and your data

- Zoonk uses AI to create plans, lessons, pictures, audio, explanations, tutor answers, and feedback. AI can make mistakes. Lessons made with AI and the AI tutor are labeled as AI.
- Your own data is never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's. That includes your chats and calls with your buddy, your memory, your uploads, lessons made just for you, and your answers. We send it only to AI providers whose terms rule out training on it.
- Lessons and pictures in the shared Library are written for many learners and hold no personal data. Some of them may be created with providers that can learn from those requests.
- For each AI request, we record the model, its cost, and how long it took, with your account ID, but never the text of the request or the answer.
- Please don't share sensitive personal data, about you or anyone else, unless your learning needs it.

---

## 4. Memory

- Zoonk remembers short facts that help it teach you, such as your goals, what you already know, and how you like to study. They come from your answers, your chats with your buddy, and your study activity.
- Memory adapts lesson examples, tutor answers, and the suggestions on Today.
- Zoonk doesn't keep health, religion, political views, or other sensitive details unless you ask it to.
- Memory is on until you turn it off. In Settings, under Memory, you can see, edit, delete, or download every fact, and turn memory off. When it's off, Zoonk doesn't learn new facts or use the ones it has.
- Deleted facts are erased within 30 days. When you delete or correct a fact, we also delete the personal lesson examples written from it.

---

## 5. Voice

- When you answer out loud, your browser or app asks for microphone access. The recording goes to an OpenAI or Google speech model to be turned into text and checked. We keep the text, not the audio.
- In live voice calls, your browser connects to OpenAI's voice model through the Vercel AI Gateway. The audio doesn't pass through our servers, and we don't store it. We keep the feedback written from the call, which may quote a few of your phrases, and its result, not the full conversation.
- The audio you hear in lessons is made by Google and OpenAI text-to-speech models from the lesson's text only.

---

## 6. Uploads

- Files you upload are stored privately and are never shown to other learners. AI models read their text so your lessons can use and cite them.
- If an upload is a public document, such as an official exam notice, we may recognize it and use it for other learners too. It is then no longer linked to your account.

---

## 7. Analytics

- We use PostHog and Vercel Analytics to understand how Zoonk is used, fix problems, and improve it. This includes session replays, which show how screens were displayed and used.
- What you type or upload may appear in a replay if it's on the screen. Only people who need it to run Zoonk can see analytics.

---

## 8. Who processes your data

We share data only as needed to run Zoonk:

- **Hosting, files, and security:** Vercel (including Vercel Blob, BotID, and the AI Gateway).
- **Database:** Neon.
- **AI:** OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google for most tasks; TypeSafe for quick text classification; Black Forest Labs and Recraft for shared Library pictures only. Other providers may step in as backups through the Vercel AI Gateway. Web searches for exam information go to Parallel and contain only search text, never your account data.
- **Analytics and errors:** PostHog, Vercel Analytics, and Sentry.
- **Email:** ZeptoMail, for sign-in codes and other account emails.
- **Payments:** Stripe on our website and Apple in the App Store.
- **Music sounds:** music activities load instrument sounds from public libraries on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees your IP address.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

These providers process data outside Brazil, mainly in the United States and the European Union, because that's needed to provide the service.

## 9. Guests

You can try Zoonk without an account. A cookie keeps your guest session on your device. Guest data is deleted after 30 days without activity, or moves to your account if you create one.

---

## 10. Your rights

Under Brazil's data-protection law (LGPD), you can ask to access, correct, download, or delete your data, learn who we share it with, withdraw consent, and ask for a review of automated decisions. You can download your data with "Download my data" on your profile page and your memory in Settings. For anything else, write to [hello@zoonk.com](mailto:hello@zoonk.com).

---

## 11. Deleting your account

- You can delete your account at any time with "Delete account" on your profile page.
- We then delete your account, learning data, memory, uploads, and anything made from your private files, and cancel your subscription on our website. A subscription bought in the App Store stays with Apple: cancel it in your Apple account.
- Afterwards, we also remove your data from our analytics, including events and session replays.
- Public documents you uploaded that became shared sources stay, since they're no longer linked to you.
- We may keep some records when the law requires it, such as payment and access logs.

---

## 12. Security

We protect your data with reasonable measures, but no system is completely secure.
