## 1. What we collect

- **Account:** your email, name, username, profile picture, birth year, language, and time zone. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we get your name and email from them, and your picture from Google.
- **Learning:** your goals, plans, lessons, answers (typed, or spoken and turned into text), progress, mistakes, time spent, feedback, and preferences such as your buddy.
- **Memory:** short facts about how you learn that Zoonk remembers to help you (see section 4).
- **What you send us:** goals you describe, chats and calls with your buddy (the AI tutor), uploaded files, pasted text, support messages, and cancellation requests.
- **Payments:** your plan and subscription status. Card details stay with the payment provider. If you end Plus for an important reason, the reason you give is kept with the payment provider.
- **Technical and usage data:** IP address, the country your connection comes from, device, browser, cookies, sign-in records, security events, and, when usage data is on (section 7), how you use Zoonk (screens, clicks or taps, and session replays). To block bots and fake apps, we run security checks on your browser, and in our apps we ask Apple or Google to confirm the app is genuine.

We collect this data electronically, from what you enter and from how you use Zoonk on the web and in our apps. You need an email address to create an account, and we ask everyone their birth year once (section 14). Everything else you give us is up to you, though some features can't work without it.

## 2. Why we use it

- **To run Zoonk for you:** your account, goals, plans, lessons, progress, buddy, memory, voice features, and account emails. We need this to provide the service you signed up for (our contract with you).
- **To check your age:** we use your birth year to apply the minimum age and to protect minors' data (section 14). Some countries require this (legal obligation); elsewhere, it's our legitimate interest in protecting young learners.
- **To take payments and keep records:** to provide Plus, handle cancellations, and send refunds (our contract with you), and to keep the tax and accounting records the law requires (legal obligation).
- **To keep Zoonk safe:** to prevent fraud, bots, and abuse, and to enforce fair-use limits. This is our legitimate interest in protecting Zoonk and the people who use it. We keep records of each sign-in because Brazilian law requires it (legal obligation, section 10).
- **To fix and improve Zoonk:** analytics, session replays, error reports, and quality checks of AI content. This is our legitimate interest in finding bugs and making Zoonk better. In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Turkey, the analytics in your browser start only if you accept them (your consent). You can turn analytics off at any time (section 7) and object to the rest (section 12).
- **If you ask Zoonk to remember something sensitive,** such as a health condition: we keep it because you asked (your explicit consent). You can delete it at any time in Settings, under Memory.
- **To build the shared Library:** we use public documents, such as official exam notices, to write lessons for many learners. This is our legitimate interest in teaching everyone well.
- **To meet legal obligations:** for example, to answer authorities when the law requires it, and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We don't show ads, and we never sell your data or share it for advertising.

## 3. AI and your data

- Zoonk uses AI to create plans, lessons, pictures, audio, explanations, tutor answers, and feedback. AI can make mistakes. Lessons made with AI and the AI tutor are labeled as AI.
- AI shapes your lessons and plan, but it doesn't make decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.
- Your own data is never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's. That includes your chats and calls with your buddy, your memory, your uploads, lessons made just for you, and your answers. We send it only to AI providers whose terms rule out training on it.
- Lessons and pictures in the shared Library are written for many learners and hold no personal data. Some of them may be created with providers that can learn from those requests.
- If a question you ask has an answer that's the same for anyone, we may share the explanation with other learners, without your words.
- For each AI request, we record the model, its cost, and how long it took, with your account ID, but not the text. The exception is some quality checks of what you write, such as questions to your buddy, which keep it for 30 days.
- Please don't share sensitive personal data, about you or anyone else, unless your learning needs it.

## 4. Memory

- Zoonk remembers short facts that help it teach you, such as your goals, what you already know, and how you like to study. They come from your answers, your chats with your buddy, and your study activity.
- Memory adapts lesson examples, tutor answers, and the suggestions on Today.
- Zoonk doesn't keep health, religion, political views, or other sensitive details unless you ask it to.
- Memory is on until you turn it off. In Settings, under Memory, you can see, edit, delete, or download every fact, and turn memory off. When it's off, Zoonk doesn't learn new facts or use the ones it has.
- Deleted facts are erased about 30 days after you delete them. When you delete or correct a fact, we also delete the personal lesson examples written from it.

---

## 5. Voice

- When you answer out loud, your browser or app asks for microphone access. The recording goes to an OpenAI or Google speech model to be turned into text and checked. We keep the text, not the audio.
- In live voice calls, your browser or app connects directly to OpenAI's voice model. The audio doesn't pass through our servers, and we don't store it. We keep the feedback written from the call, which may quote a few of your phrases, and its result, not the full conversation.
- The audio you hear in lessons is made from the lesson's text only, by Google text-to-speech models, with Microsoft's as a backup.

---

## 6. Uploads

- Files you upload are stored privately and are never shown to other learners. AI models read their text so your lessons can use and cite them.
- If an upload is a public document, such as an official exam notice, we may recognize it and use it for other learners too. It is then no longer linked to your account. Like other shared Library content, it may then go to AI providers that can learn from it (section 3).

## 7. Analytics and cookies

- We use PostHog (analytics and session replays) and Vercel Web Analytics to understand how Zoonk is used, fix problems, and improve it. A session replay shows how screens were displayed and used. What you type or upload may appear in a replay if it's on the screen. Only people who need it to run Zoonk can see analytics.
- **When they start:** In the EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Turkey, they wait until you choose "Accept" in our notice about usage data. Elsewhere, they're on unless you turn them off. Our sign-in pages don't load them.
- **Turning them off:** Use "Share usage data" in Settings › Preferences, on the web or in our apps. Turning it off stops the analytics in your browser and the events our servers record about your account. If you turn it off there on any of your devices, it's off for your account everywhere.
- **Minors:** We record session replays and keep a PostHog profile only for learners we know are 18 or older, or 19 or older in Korea. For anyone younger, or whose age we don't know, there are no replays and no profile.
- **Events from our servers:** Our servers also record some events, such as finishing a lesson or how you signed in, to understand how Zoonk is used. They don't use cookies and rely on our legitimate interest. Turning off "Share usage data" stops them too. Our apps don't include analytics tools, so only these events apply there.
- **Your country:** On each visit, we work out your country from your IP address, or from your browser's language when that's not available, to show prices and apply your country's rules. We don't save it to your account.

Cookies and similar storage on your device:

- **Sign-in:** keep you signed in or keep your guest session, until you sign out or after 30 days without use. Strictly necessary.
- **`ZOONK_LOCALE`:** remembers your language, for 12 months. Strictly necessary.
- **`ZOONK_COUNTRY`:** remembers your country, to know whether to ask about usage data, for 1 hour. Strictly necessary.
- **`ZOONK_USAGE_DATA`:** remembers your choice about usage data, for 12 months. Strictly necessary.
- **`ZOONK_AGE_STOP`:** after a birth year under the minimum age, keeps this device from trying again, for 24 hours. Strictly necessary.
- **PostHog (`ph_*`):** recognizes your visits for analytics. Only after you accept, where that's required.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block all of them, you can't stay signed in.

Your browser or app sends information directly to these services:

- **PostHog:** when usage data is on, the screens you see, clicks and taps, a visitor ID, device, browser, IP address, and session replays, to understand use and fix problems.
- **Vercel Web Analytics:** when usage data is on, the page address, the page you came from, device, and browser, to count visits.
- **Vercel BotID:** security checks when you sign in, start as a guest, or cancel a subscription, to block bots.
- **Vercel Blob:** the files you upload and your IP address, when you upload, to store them (section 6).
- **OpenAI:** your voice, IP address, and browser or device, during live voice calls, to run the call (section 5).
- **Sentry:** error details, device, and browser, to fix errors.
- **jsDelivr:** your IP address and browser, in coding activities, to load the coding tools.
- **GitHub Pages:** your IP address and browser, in music activities, to load instrument sounds.

## 10. How long we keep data

- **Account and learning data,** including your birth year: while your account exists. We delete it when you delete your account (see section 13).
- **Guest data:** until 30 days without activity, or until it moves to your account.
- **Memory facts you delete:** erased about 30 days after you delete them.
- **AI quality checks of what you write,** such as questions to your buddy: 30 days.
- **Requests to AI providers:** some providers keep them for a limited time, as their terms allow, to detect abuse, then delete them.
- **Sign-in codes:** they expire after 5 minutes and are deleted within 2 days.
- **Sessions:** the IP address and device of each session, until you sign out, the session expires after 30 days without use, or you delete your account. Expired sessions are deleted every day.
- **Sign-in records:** the account ID, IP address, and time of each sign-in, including guest sessions, for 6 calendar months, also after you delete your account, as Brazilian law requires (Marco Civil da Internet, art. 15). They're then deleted in our daily cleanup.
- **Error reports:** deleted automatically by Sentry within 90 days.
- **Analytics and session replays:** until we remove them after you delete your account (section 13), or until PostHog's retention period ends, whichever is sooner.
- **Your choice about usage data:** on your account while it exists, and in a cookie on your device for 12 months.
- **Cost records of AI requests and calls:** kept for our accounts. They never hold what you said or wrote. When you delete your account, we keep them without your account ID.
- **Feedback you send:** kept to improve Zoonk, without your email once you delete your account. Copies of feedback and support emails stay in our mailbox as long as we need them to answer you and handle any claim.
- **Payment records:** kept by Stripe, Apple, or Google as payment and tax law requires, including the reason you give for ending Plus for an important reason.
- **Backups:** deleted data stays in our database provider's recovery history for up to 30 days, then it's gone.

When data reaches the end of its period, we delete it from our database and file storage. It can't be recovered after that.

## 13. Deleting your account

- You can delete your account at any time with "Delete account" on your profile page.
- We then delete your account, learning data, memory, uploads, and anything made from your private files, and end your subscription on our website right away, without a refund. To get a refund you're owed, such as the 14-day refund, cancel before you delete your account, or write to us afterward from the same email (see the [Terms](/terms)). A subscription bought in the App Store or Google Play stays with Apple or Google: cancel it in your account there.
- Some study statistics, such as whether a question was answered correctly the first time and how long a lesson took, are kept without anything that links them to you, to improve lessons for everyone.
- Within 30 days, we also delete your PostHog profile, with its events and session replays. Events without a profile, such as those of learners under 18, keep your account ID but no name or email, and are deleted when PostHog's retention period ends.
- Sign-in records stay for 6 months (section 10).
- Public documents you uploaded that became shared sources stay, since they're no longer linked to you.
- Payment records stay with Stripe, Apple, or Google, as payment and tax law requires.

## 14. Age and children

- Zoonk is for people 13 and older, or 14 and older in Italy and Korea. Teenagers under 18, or under 19 in Korea, need a parent's or guardian's permission.
- We ask everyone the year they were born, once, before they can go on: a visitor before their guest session starts, and anyone with an account who hasn't told us yet. We keep the year, never the full date, while your account exists. You can't change it yourself; if it's wrong, write to us.
- The minimum age is the one where you are when you answer, and we count the youngest age your birth year allows.
- If you might be under the minimum age, we don't save the year: a visitor's guest session doesn't start, and an account is logged out. This device can't try again for 24 hours.
- We record no session replays and keep no analytics profile for learners under 18, or under 19 in Korea (section 7).
- If you believe a child under the minimum age gave us data, write to [hello@zoonk.com](mailto:hello@zoonk.com) and we'll delete it.

## 15. Security

- Data travels between your device, Zoonk, and our providers over encrypted connections.
- Our database and file storage providers encrypt data at rest. Uploads are stored privately.
- We store sign-in codes only as scrambled values (hashes), so they can't be read back.
- Only people who need it to run Zoonk can access personal data, each with their own account.
- No system is completely secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
