## Patterns

- **Factual errors taught as right:** B in pair 5 (the p-value read as the chance the gap is luck). A in pair 2 ("únicas formas") and pair 3 (the wrong distractor rationale). A in pair 9 overclaims from a null result, and in pair 8 it fills blanks from an unverified assumption.
- **Broken or empty screens:** only pair 3 A.
- **Decision quality in challenge cases:** where both sides were accurate, the case with more and less predictable choices usually won. The careful but thin sides (B in pairs 8, 9 and 10) lost on decisions, not on facts.
- **Lesson fixes:** both sides fixed what was asked in all six pairs, and both rejected the wrong citation request in pair 11. Wins came only from unrequested edits (pair 12 B) and check quality (pair 16 B). The rest are ties.
