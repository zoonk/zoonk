# `es` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use Latin American Spanish, with Mexico as the reference variety, worded so it reads naturally anywhere in Latin America and among US Spanish speakers: no slang or words only Mexico uses (“chido”, “padre”, “ahorita”, “platicar”, “checar”).
- Informal “tú” for one person and “ustedes” for a group; never “vosotros” or “vos”.
- For what just happened, use the preterite: “Dijiste”, not “Has dicho”; “No pudimos…”, not “No hemos podido…”.
- Use Latin American words, not Spain's: “computadora”, “celular”, “jugo”, “boleto”, “departamento”, “video”, “lentes”, “Configuración” (settings), “ingresar” (type in), “administrar” (manage), not “ordenador”, “móvil”, “zumo”, “billete”, “piso”, “vídeo”, “gafas”, “Ajustes”, “introducir”, “gestionar”. Never “vale” or “coger”.
- Buttons, links and menu actions use the infinitive: “Crear una cuenta gratis”, not “Crea una cuenta gratis”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for Mexico, such as the EXANI-II and the UNAM's admission exam.
- Treat “Poder Mental” as a product name without an article in short labels and headings.

## Glossary

- “goal”: “objetivo”; “start a goal”: “crear un objetivo”, not “empezar un objetivo”
- “check” (an answer): “comprobar”
- “guess” (a pick that doesn't count): “suposición”, never “respuesta”
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “preparar”; learner writing: “escribir”
- “learning” (study days): “estudio”
- “Brain Power”: “Poder Mental”
- “score”, including an exam's: “puntaje”, not “puntuación” or “nota”; the admission cut-off: “puntaje mínimo”
- “grade” (an essay): “calificar”, and the result “calificación”, not “corregir” or “nota”; “fix” (a mistake): “corregir”
- “band” (an exam's score band): “banda”
- “subject” (of an exam or a plan): “materia”, not “asignatura”
- “prerequisite”: “requisito previo”, not “conocimiento previo”
- “yet” / “still”: “aún”, not “todavía”
- “keep” before a verb (“Keep learning”): “seguir”, not “continuar”
- Language skills “Listening” / “Speaking” / “Reading” / “Writing” / “Words”: “Escucha” / “Expresión oral” / “Lectura” / “Expresión escrita” / “Vocabulario”
- “Use of language” (a speaking exam criterion): “Uso de la lengua”
- “card”: “tarjeta”, not “carta”
- “tap” (the screen): “tocar”; a key: “presionar”, not “pulsar”
- “bpm” (a tempo): “bpm”, not “ppm”
- “link” (between a cause and its effect): “relación”, not “conexión”
- “Free” (subscription name): “Gratis”
- “Get Plus”: “obtener Plus”, not “pasarse a Plus” or “hacerse Plus”
- “hello@zoonk.com”: “contacto@zoonk.com”
- “the App Store”: “el App Store”, as Apple writes it in Latin America
- “You've reached today's study time”: “Completaste tu tiempo de estudio de hoy”, in every line that starts with it

## Feature names and states

- “Buddy”: “Compañero”
- Mission names (missions list, first steps): “tú” imperative: “Completa tu primera lección”
- “essay”: “redacción”, not “ensayo”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “Cuaderno de errores”
- “Saved to your mistakes”: “Guardado en tu cuaderno de errores”, in every line that starts with it
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “Por corregir” / “Corregidos”
- “full review” (language exam preparation): “revisión completa”; spaced review: “repaso”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “Nuevo” / “Aprendiendo” / “Firme” / “Dominado”
- “Known before”: “Ya conocidos”; “Known” (progress legend): “Ya lo sabías”
- “Preparation”: “Preparación”
- “the Trickster”: “el Tramposo”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “Roto” / “Resistió”
- “Phase challenge”: “Desafío de la fase”, not “reto”, “control”, or “examen”
- “Weekly challenge”: “Desafío semanal”
- “Mock exam”, including speaking mocks: “Simulacro”; standalone title: “Simulacro de examen”
- “Logbook”: “Bitácora”
- “Fading” (needs review): “Por repasar”
- “Overview” (course level): “Panorama general”
- “Focus test”: “Prueba de enfoque”

## Plan terminology

- “Finished”: “Completado”
- “Past date”: “Fecha pasada”, not “Terminado”, “Caducado”, or “Vencido”
- “Needs adjusting”: “Necesita ajustes”
- “Too tight”: “Demasiado ajustado”
- “Even across skills”: “Equilibrado en todas las habilidades”
- “left out”: “omitir”, not “saltar”
- “On pace” / “Keeping pace”: “Al día”, not “A buen ritmo”
- “Daily time”: “Tiempo diario”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “Coco”
- “Beep”: “Bip”
- “Otto”: “Oto”

## Formatting preference

- Write numbers the Mexican way, with no space before “%”: “1,234.56”, “50%”.
- Match the app's compact durations: “2 h 30 min”, “30 s”.
- “About {time}”: “aproximadamente {time}”, not “unos {time}”.
- Quote with “”, not «».
- Keys “Enter” / “Shift”: “Enter” / “Shift”.
