# `es` styleguide

- Use Spain Spanish (es-ES) spelling, grammar, and vocabulary. For example, "ordenador" (not "computadora") and "móvil" (not "celular").
- Use an informal, approachable register. Address the user as "tú", and use "vosotros" (not "ustedes") for an informal plural "you".

## Special Cases

- Treat "Poder Mental" as a product-like term in short labels and headings. For example, write "¿Qué es Poder Mental?" instead of adding a leading article.
- Use an article or possessive with "Poder Mental" only when the sentence sounds more natural with one. For example, "Has aumentado tu Poder Mental".
- Study cards are "tarjetas", never "cartas".
- Use Spain's context for examples and exam names, such as "oposiciones" and the "PAU", instead of Latin American or US ones.
- When the Trickster's name starts a sentence or stands alone as a title, capitalize its article: "El Tramposo".
- Statements in exams where a wrong answer cancels a right one are judged right or wrong, not true or false: "{judgment, select, right {Right} other {Wrong}}" = "{judgment, select, right {Correcto} other {Incorrecto}}". Other uses of "right" and "wrong" keep their usual translations.
