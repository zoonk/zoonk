# `es` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use Spain Spanish (es-ES), informal “tú” and “vosotros”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for Spain, such as oposiciones and the PAU.
- Treat “Poder Mental” as a product name without an article in short labels and headings.

## Glossary

- “goal”: “objetivo”
- “session” (study): “sesión”
- “check” (an answer): “comprobar”
- “guess” (a pick that doesn't count, “Your guess”): “suposición”, never “respuesta”
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “preparar”; learner writing: “escribir”
- “learning” (study days): “estudio”
- “Brain Power”: “Poder Mental”
- “BP”: “PM”
- “listening”: “escucha”
- “Speaking”: “Expresión oral”
- “card”: “tarjeta”, not “carta”
- “hello@zoonk.com”: “contacto@zoonk.com”

## Feature names and states

- “Buddy”: “Compañero”
- “Time capsules”: “Cápsulas del tiempo”
- Mission names (missions list, first steps): “tú” imperative: “Completa tu primera lección”, “Practica {area}”
- “essay”: “redacción”, not “ensayo”
- “Hyperdrive”: “Hiperimpulso”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “Cuaderno de errores”
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “Por corregir” / “Corregidos”
- “full review” (language exam preparation): “revisión completa”; spaced review: “repaso”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “Nuevo” / “Aprendiendo” / “Firme” / “Dominado”
- “Known before”: “Ya conocidos”; “Known” (progress legend): “Ya lo sabías”
- “Preparation”: “Preparación”
- “Boss”: “Jefe final”
- “the Trickster”: “el Tramposo”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “Roto” / “Ha resistido”
- “Big Challenge”: “Gran Desafío”
- “Phase challenge”: “Desafío de la fase”, not “reto”, “control”, or “examen”
- “Weekly challenge”: “Desafío semanal”
- “Mock exam”, including speaking mocks: “Simulacro”; standalone title: “Simulacro de examen”
- “Full meal”: “Comida completa”
- “Logbook”: “Cuaderno de bitácora”
- “Fading” (needs review): “Por repasar”
- “Overview” (course level): “Visión general”
- “Focus test”: “Prueba de enfoque”

## Plan terminology

- “Finished”: “Completado”
- “Past date”: “Fecha pasada”, not “Terminado”, “Caducado”, or “Vencido”
- “Needs adjusting”: “Necesita ajustes”
- “Too tight”: “Demasiado ajustado”
- “Even across skills”: “Igualado en todas las habilidades”
- “left out”: “omitir”, not “saltar”
- “Daily time”: “Tiempo diario”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “Coco”
- “Beep”: “Bip”
- “Otto”: “Oto”

## Formatting preference

- Match the app's compact durations: “2 h 30 min”, “30 s”.
