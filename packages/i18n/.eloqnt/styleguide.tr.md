# `tr` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use Turkish for Turkey and informal “sen”.
- Buttons, links, menu actions and mission names use the “sen” imperative: “Tekrar dene”, “Yeni bir hedefe başla”, “İlk dersini bitir”.
- Lines saying what Zoonk is doing while it works speak as “biz” (“3. sayfayı hazırlıyoruz…”). The buddy speaks as “ben”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for Turkey, such as YKS, LGS and KPSS.

## Placeholders

- Never attach a suffix to a name, title or date placeholder, since its vowel harmony is unknown. Rephrase so a noun or postposition carries it: “{buddy} ile”, “{date} tarihinde”.
- Compact durations end with a period (“30 dk.”), so a sentence ending with a duration placeholder takes no second one.

## Glossary

- “lesson”: “ders”
- “chapter”: “ünite”, not “bölüm” (a speaking task)
- “goal” (the learner's goal): “hedef”
- “plan” (study plan): “plan”; subscription plan: “paket”
- “Free” (subscription name): “Ücretsiz”
- “Get Plus”: “Plus’a geç”
- “session” (study): “çalışma” (“bugünkü çalışma”), not “oturum”, which is the sign-in session or a mock's section
- “subject” of an exam: “branş”, not “ders” (a lesson); a subject the learner studies as a goal, rather than an exam or a language: “konu”
- “topic”: “konu”
- “area” (a weighted part of an exam or a goal): “alan”
- “section” (a part of a mock exam): “oturum”
- “material” (the learner's own files and notes): “materyal”
- “notice” (an exam's official notice): “ilan”; an official guide: “kılavuz”
- “position” (the post an exam is for): “kadro”, never “konum”
- “check” (an answer): “kontrol etmek”, as in the “Kontrol et” button
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “hazırlamak”; learner writing: “yazmak”
- “essay”: “kompozisyon”, not “deneme” (a mock exam)
- “guess” (a pick that doesn't count) and the learner's “estimate” before the answer shows: “tahmin”, never “cevap”
- “say” (aloud, in pronunciation practice): “söylemek”, as in “Sesli söyle”
- “share” (personal facts): “anlatmak”
- “task” (a speaking exam's task): “bölüm”, not “görev” (a mission); its task card: “kart”
- “hand in” (a mock or one of its parts): “teslim etmek”
- “flag” (a mock question to come back to): “işaretlemek”
- “pattern” (a mistake that recurs): “tekrarlayan hata”
- “link” (between a cause and its effect): “ilişki” (“neden-sonuç ilişkisi”), not “bağlantı”
- “score”, including an exam's “band”: “puan”, not “not” (an essay's grade)
- “memory” (what Zoonk remembers about the learner): “bellek”; the learner remembering what they studied: “hafıza”
- Language skills “Listening” / “Speaking” / “Reading” / “Writing” / “Words”: “Dinleme” / “Konuşma” / “Okuma” / “Yazma” / “Kelime bilgisi”
- Speaking criteria “Fluency” / “Accuracy” / “Interaction” / “Range of language”: “Akıcılık” / “Doğruluk” / “Etkileşim” / “İfade zenginliği”
- “examiner” / “another candidate” (the AI's roles in a speaking exam): “değerlendirici” / “diğer aday”
- “tap” (a beat the learner taps in a rhythm activity): “vuruş”; the verb: “dokun”
- “prerequisite”: “ön bilgi”, not “ön koşul”
- “musicianship”: “müzik temelleri”
- “study time”, the daily amount: “çalışma süresi”; the time of day the learner studies: “çalışma saati”
- “You've reached today's study time”: “Bugünkü çalışma süreni doldurdun”, in every line that starts with it
- Ending a subscription “for an important reason”: “haklı bir nedenle”
- “Keep Plus” (turns a stopped renewal back on): “Plus’ı koru”

## Feature names and states

- “Buddy”: “Arkadaş”; “study buddy”: “ders arkadaşı”
- Buddy stages “Baby” / “Young” / “Adult”: “Bebek” / “Genç” / “Yetişkin”
- “Brain Power”: “Beyin Gücü”
- “mission”: “görev”
- “call” (speaking practice): “sohbet”, not “arama”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “Yanlış defteri”
- “Saved to your mistakes”: “Yanlış defterine kaydedildi”, in every line that starts with it
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “Düzeltilecekler” / “Düzeltilenler”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “Yeni” / “Öğreniliyor” / “Sağlam” / “Tam öğrenildi”
- “Known before”: “Zaten biliyordun”; “Known” (progress legend): “Biliniyor”
- “Preparation”: “Hazırlık”
- “the Trickster”: “Hilebaz”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “Kırıldı” / “Dayandı”
- “Phase challenge”: “Aşama meydan okuması”
- “Weekly challenge”: “Haftalık meydan okuma”
- “Mock exam”, including speaking mocks: “deneme sınavı”, never just “deneme”, which is a try
- “Logbook”: “Çalışma günlüğü”
- “Fading” (needs review): “Tekrar edilecek”
- “Overview” (course level): “Genel bakış”
- “Focus test”: “Odak testi”
- “Weekly summary”: “Haftalık özet”
- “Full review”: “Genel tekrar”; spaced review: “tekrar”
- Belts: “kuşak”, not “kemer”; “Purple belt”: “Mor kuşak”

## Plan terminology

- “Pace”: “Tempo”; the “On pace” / “Keeping pace” status: “Yolunda”
- “Finished”: “Tamamlandı”
- “Past date”: “Hedef tarih geçti”, not “Süresi doldu”, “Tarihi geçti” or “Bitti”
- “Needs adjusting”: “Ayarlanmalı”
- “Too tight”: “Çok sıkışık”
- “Even across skills”: “Beceriler dengeli”
- “left out”: “dışarıda kaldı”, not “atlandı”
- “Skip” (the learner's choice) and “skipped” (a test showed it's known): “atla” / “atlandı”
- “Daily time”: “Günlük çalışma süresi”
- “rest day”: “dinlenme günü”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “Beyno”
- “Beep”: “Bip”
- “Otto”: “Otto”

## Formatting preferences

- Match the app's compact durations: “2 sa. 30 dk.”, “30 sn.”.
- Use the typographic apostrophe (’) before a suffix: “Zoonk’ta”.
