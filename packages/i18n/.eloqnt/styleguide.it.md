# `it` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use Italian for Italy and informal “tu”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for Italy, such as the Maturità, university admission tests and concorsi pubblici.
- Lines saying what Zoonk is doing while it works speak as “noi” (“Stiamo preparando la pagina 3…”). The buddy speaks as “io”.
- Buttons, links, menu actions and mission names use the “tu” imperative: “Riprova”, “Inizia un nuovo obiettivo”, “Completa la tua prima lezione”.
- Avoid adjectives and participles that mark the learner's or the buddy's gender, also in lines they say: “Ti diamo il benvenuto”, not “Benvenuto”. Never use “ə”, “*” or “/a”.
- Treat “Potenza Mentale” as a product name without an article in short labels and headings. Amounts in sentences take “punti”: “500 punti di Potenza Mentale”.

## Glossary

- “goal” (the learner's goal): “obiettivo”
- “plan” (study plan): “piano”, not “piano di studi” (a degree's course list) or “programma”; subscription plan: “formula” (“la formula Free”), never “piano”
- “Free” (subscription name): “Free”
- “Get Plus”: “passa a Plus”
- “subject” (of an exam or a plan): “materia”
- “topic”: “argomento”, not “tema”
- “area” (a weighted part of an exam or a goal): “area”
- “material” (the learner's own files and notes): “materiale”, never “materia”
- “notice” (an exam's official notice): “bando”
- “check” (an answer): “controllare”, as in the “Controlla” button
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “preparare”; learner writing: “scrivere”
- “essay”: “tema”, not “saggio”
- “guess” (a pick that doesn't count): “ipotesi”, never “risposta”; the verb: “indovinare”
- “say” (aloud, in pronunciation practice): “dire”, as in “Dillo ad alta voce”
- “share” (personal facts): “raccontare”
- “task” (a speaking exam's task): “compito”
- “card”: a speaking exam's task card: “scheda”; a card to pick in an activity: “carta”
- “hand in” (a mock or one of its parts): “consegnare”
- “pattern” (a mistake that recurs): “errore ricorrente”
- “link” (between a cause and its effect): “nesso”, not “collegamento”
- “score”, including an exam's “band”: “punteggio”, not “voto” (an essay's grade)
- Language skills “Listening” / “Speaking” / “Reading” / “Writing” / “Words”: “Ascolto” / “Parlato” / “Lettura” / “Scrittura” / “Lessico”
- Speaking criteria “Fluency” / “Accuracy” / “Interaction” / “Range of language”: “Fluidità” / “Correttezza” / “Interazione” / “Ricchezza linguistica”
- “tap” (a beat the learner taps in a rhythm activity): “colpo”; the verb: “tocca”
- “musicianship”: “formazione musicale”
- “study time”, the daily amount: “tempo di studio”; the time of day the learner studies: “orario di studio”
- “You've reached today's study time”: “Hai esaurito il tempo di studio di oggi”, in every line that starts with it

## Feature names and states

- “Buddy”: “Compagno”; “study buddy”: “compagno di studio”
- Buddy stages “Baby” / “Young” / “Adult”: “Piccolo” / “Giovane” / “Adulto”
- “mission”: “missione”
- “call” (speaking practice): “conversazione”, not “chiamata”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “Quaderno degli errori”
- “Saved to your mistakes”: “Salvato nel tuo quaderno degli errori”, in every line that starts with it
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “Da correggere” / “Corretti”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “Nuovo” / “In corso” / “Solido” / “Padroneggiato”
- “Known before”: “Lo sapevi già”; “Known” (progress legend): “Già noto”
- “Preparation”: “Preparazione”
- “the Trickster”: “l’Ingannatore”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “Rotto” / “Ha retto”
- “Phase challenge”: “Sfida della fase”
- “Weekly challenge”: “Sfida settimanale”
- “Mock exam”, including speaking mocks: “Simulazione”; standalone title: “Simulazione d’esame”
- “Logbook”: “Diario di bordo”
- “Fading” (needs review): “Da ripassare”
- “Overview” (course level): “Panoramica”
- “Focus test”: “Test delle priorità”
- “Weekly summary”: “Riepilogo settimanale”
- “Full review”: “Ripasso generale”
- Belts: “cintura”; “Purple belt”: “Cintura viola”, not “porpora”

## Plan terminology

- “Pace”: “Ritmo”; the “On pace” / “Keeping pace” status: “Al passo”
- “Finished”: “Completato”
- “Past date”: “Data superata”, not “Scaduto” or “Terminato”
- “Needs adjusting”: “Da adattare”
- “Too tight”: “Troppo serrato”
- “Even across skills”: “Equilibrato tra le abilità”
- “left out”: “lasciato fuori”, not “saltato”
- “Skip” (the learner's choice) and “skipped” (a test showed it's known): “saltare” / “saltato”
- “Daily time”: “Tempo giornaliero”
- “rest day”: “giorno di riposo”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “Zucca”
- “Beep”: “Bip”
- “Otto”: “Otto”

## Formatting preferences

- Match the app's compact durations: “2 h 30 min”, “30 s”.
- Use the typographic apostrophe (’), never the straight one ('), which can break a placeholder that follows it.
