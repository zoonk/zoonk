# `de` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use German for Germany and informal “du”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for Germany, such as the Abitur.
- Treat “Brain Power” as a product name without an article in short labels and headings.
- Lines saying what Zoonk is doing while it works, titles included (“Writing screen 3…”, “Building your plan”), speak as “wir” (“Wir erstellen Seite 3…”, “Wir erstellen deinen Plan”), never as an infinitive or an imperative to the learner. The buddy speaks as “ich”.
- Durations come with their abbreviation's period (“15 Min.”, “1 Std.”): a sentence ending with a duration placeholder (`{time}`, or an Android `%1$s` that holds a duration) ends there, without a second period: “Tägliche Lernzeit: %1$s”. Every other sentence keeps its period.
- Role examples stay roles, with a neutral noun where German has one (“Pflegekraft”, “Lehrkraft”), never a field (“Pflege”, “Datenanalyse”).

## Glossary

- “lesson”: “Lektion”
- “goal” (the learner's goal): “Ziel”, not “Lernziel”
- “plan” (study plan): “Plan”, also in a sentence about Plus (“every week in your plan”, “your plan and progress”); subscription plan (“the free plan”, “as your plan allows”): “Tarif”
- “Free” (subscription name): “Free”
- “session” (study): “Lerneinheit”; sign-in session: “Sitzung”; a course “unit”: “Einheit”
- “subject” (of an exam or a plan): “Fach”; “topic”: “Thema”; “area”: “Bereich”
- “notice” (an exam's official notice): “Ausschreibung”
- “dates” of phases or exams: “Termine” (“Phasentermine”), since “Daten” reads as data
- “prerequisite”: “Voraussetzung”; “the basics”: “Grundlagen”
- “screen” (a lesson's page): “Seite”, not “Bildschirm”
- “check” (an answer): “prüfen”
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “erstellen”; learner writing: “schreiben”
- “Brain Power”: “Brain Power”
- “BP”: “BP”
- “score”: “Score”, not “Bewertung” or “Punktzahl”
- “band” (an exam's score band): “Bandwert”
- “level”: “Level”, not “Niveau”
- “start over” (answers or a plan): “von vorn beginnen”; “neu starten” is restarting a device or running code again
- “position” (the post an exam is for): “Stelle”
- “listening”: “Hören”
- “Speaking”: “Sprechen”
- “card”: “Karte”, not “Lernkarte”; a speaking exam's task card may be “Aufgabenkarte”
- “in a row” (right answers in a streak): “hintereinander”, not “in Folge”
- “again”, including “Retry” and “Rematch”: “noch einmal”, not “erneut”
- “in a moment” (try again): “gleich”, not “in einem Moment”
- “one goal at a time”: “jeweils ein Ziel”
- “cover” (time or a plan covering the goal): “abdecken”, not “bearbeiten”
- “Ask about …” (to the buddy): “Frag etwas zu …”, “Frag {name} etwas zu …”
- “tap” (a beat the learner taps in a rhythm activity): “Schlag”, never “Tipp”, which is a hint
- “examiner” / “another candidate” (the AI's roles in a speaking exam): “prüfende Person” / “andere teilnehmende Person”
- Speaking criteria “Fluency” / “Accuracy” / “Interaction”: “Redefluss” / “Korrektheit” / “Interaktion”
- “musicianship”: “musikalische Grundlagen”
- Imperatives, activity chips included, use the short “du” form without the final “-e” wherever German allows it: “Versuch es noch einmal”, “Frag”, “Stell eine Frage”, “Schreib”, “Trag”, “Zieh”, “Schließ”, “Erklär”, “Schätz zuerst”, not “Versuche”, “Frage”, “Stelle”, “Schreibe”; keep the “-e” only where German requires it (“Antworte”, “Öffne”)

- “Hyperdrive”: “Hyperantrieb”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “Fehlerheft”
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “Noch offen” / “Korrigiert”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “Neu” / “Im Aufbau” / “Sicher” / “Gemeistert”
- “Known before”: “Schon bekannt”; “Known” (progress legend): “Bekannt”
- “Preparation”: “Vorbereitung”
- “Boss”: “Endgegner”
- “the Trickster”: “der Trickser”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “Zerbrochen” / “Gehalten”
- “Big Challenge”: “Große Herausforderung”
- “Phase challenge”: “Phasen-Herausforderung”
- “Weekly challenge”: “Wöchentliche Herausforderung”

## Plan terminology

- “Pace”: “Tempo”; the “On pace” / “Keeping pace” status: “Im Zeitplan”
- “Finished”: “Abgeschlossen”
- “Past date”: “Nach dem Zieldatum”, not “Beendet” or “Abgelaufen”
- “Needs adjusting”: “Anpassung nötig”
- “Too tight”: “Zu knapp”
- “Even across skills”: “Alle Fähigkeiten gleichauf”
- “left out”: “ausgelassen”, not “übersprungen”
- “Daily time” and “Time a day”: “Tägliche Lernzeit”, not “Zeitbudget” or “Zeit pro Tag”
- “rest day”: “Pausentag”, not “Ruhetag”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “Grips”
- “Beep”: “Piep”
- “Otto”: “Otto”

## Formatting preference

- Match the app's compact durations: “2 Std. 30 Min.”, “30 Sek.”.
- A date placeholder takes “am” or its article (“am {date}”, “der {date}”); a month, with or without the year, takes “im” (“im {month}”).
- A compound with a multi-word name is hyphenated throughout: “App-Store-Abo”, “Google-Play-Kauf”, “Zoonk-App”.
