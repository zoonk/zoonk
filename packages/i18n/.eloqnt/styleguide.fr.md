# `fr` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use French for France and informal “tu”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for France, such as the bac.
- Treat “Puissance Mentale” as a product name without an article in short labels and headings. Amounts take “points”: “500 points de Puissance Mentale”.

## Glossary

- “plan” (study plan): “plan”, not “programme” or “parcours”; subscription plan: “formule”
- “Free” (subscription): “gratuite”
- “Guardian”: “Parent ou tuteur”
- “session” (study): “séance”; sign-in session: “session”
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “préparer”; learner writing: “écrire”
- “share” (personal facts): “dire”
- “Brain Power”: “Puissance Mentale”
- “BP”: “PM”
- “score”, including an exam's “band”: “Score”, not “note” or “bande”
- Language skills “Listening” / “Speaking” / “Reading” / “Words”: “Écoute” / “Expression orale” / “Lecture” / “Vocabulaire”
- Speaking criteria “Fluency” / “Accuracy” / “Range of language”: “Aisance” / “Précision” / “Richesse de la langue”
- “say” (aloud, in pronunciation practice): “dire”, as in “Dis-le à voix haute”
- “task” (a speaking exam's task): “tâche”, not “exercice”
- “flagged” (a mock exam question to come back to): “marquée”, not “signalée”
- “pattern” (a mistake that recurs): “erreur récurrente”
- “mind map”: “carte mentale”
- “topic”: “sujet”, not “thème”
- “tap”: “appuie sur”, not “touche”
- “card”: a speaking exam's task card: “fiche”; a card to pick in an activity: “carte”
- “subject” (of an exam, a goal or a plan): “matière”, not “sujet” (a topic)
- “area” (a weighted part of an exam or a goal): “domaine”, not “matière”
- “notice” (an exam's official notice): “avis” or “avis d’examen”, never “notice”
- “material” (the learner's own files and notes): “documents”, “contenu” or “support”, never “matière”
- “Send feedback” (to Zoonk): “Donner ton avis”, not “Envoyer un retour” or “Envoyer un avis”

## Feature names and states

- “Buddy”: “Compagnon”
- “Time capsules”: “Capsules temporelles”
- Mission names (missions list, first steps): infinitive: “Terminer ta première leçon”; “Practice” / “Review”: “S’entraîner” / “Réviser”
- “essay”: “rédaction”, not “dissertation”
- “Hyperdrive”: “Hyperpropulsion”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “Carnet d’erreurs”
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “À corriger” / “Corrigées”
- “practice mistakes”: “réviser tes erreurs”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “Nouveau” / “En cours” / “Solide” / “Maîtrisé”
- “Known before”: “Déjà connus”; “Known” (progress legend): “Déjà acquis”
- “Preparation”: “Préparation”
- “Boss”: “Boss”
- “the Trickster”: “le Farceur”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “Brisé” / “A tenu”
- “Big Challenge”: “Grand Défi”
- “Phase challenge”: “Défi de la phase”, not “contrôle”, “bilan”, or “étape”
- “Weekly challenge”: “Défi hebdomadaire”
- “Mock exam”, including speaking mocks: “Examen blanc”, not “simulation”
- “Full meal”: “Repas complet”
- “Logbook”: “Journal de bord”
- “Fading” (needs review): “À revoir”
- “Overview” (course level): “Vue d’ensemble”
- “Focus test”: “Test de priorités”
- “Weekly summary”: “Bilan de la semaine”

## Plan terminology

- “Finished”: “Terminé”
- “Past date”: “Date passée”, not “Clos” or “Expiré”
- “Needs adjusting”: “À ajuster”
- “Too tight”: “Trop serré”
- “Even across skills”: “Équilibré entre les compétences”
- “left out”: “laissé de côté”, not “passé” or “sauté”
- “Skip” (the learner's choice) and “skipped” (a test showed it's known): “sauter” / “sauté”, never “passer” or “passé”
- “Daily time”: “Durée quotidienne”, not “Temps par jour”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “Caboche”
- “Beep”: “Bip”
- “Otto”: “Octave”

## Formatting preferences

- Match the app's compact durations: “2 h 30 min”, “30 s”.
- Use a no-break space (U+00A0) before “?”, “!”, “;”, and “:” and inside « ».
- Use the typographic apostrophe (’), never the straight one (').
