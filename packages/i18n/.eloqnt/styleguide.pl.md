# `pl` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use Polish for Poland and informal “ty”, lowercase mid-sentence: “twój plan”.
- Buttons, links, menu actions and mission names use the “ty” imperative: “Spróbuj ponownie”, “Ukończ pierwszą lekcję”.
- Lines saying what Zoonk is doing while it works speak as “my” (“Przygotowujemy stronę 3…”). The buddy speaks as “ja”.
- The learner's and the buddy's gender are unknown, and Polish marks them in past-tense verbs of any person and in adjectives about them (“pewny”, “gotowy”). Use the present or future, “udało ci/mi się”, impersonal “-no” forms or a noun phrase: “Lekcja ukończona!”, “Nie wiem”, “Zmiana jest poniżej”, not “Ukończyłeś lekcję!”, “Ukończyłeś(-aś)” or “Nie jestem pewny”.
- Decline “Zoonk” as a masculine noun: “w Zoonku”, “z Zoonkiem”.
- Localize an exam named only as an example, such as a goal suggestion, for Poland: the matura and the egzamin ósmoklasisty. Keep the exam when the screen shows its own scores, sections or questions, such as the SAT example plan.
- Treat “Moc Umysłu” as a product name. Amounts take “pkt”: “+10 pkt Mocy Umysłu”.

## Placeholders

- Name and title placeholders can't decline: keep them in the nominative, as the subject or after a colon: “Napisz do: {name}”.

## Glossary

- “goal” (the learner's goal): “cel”
- “plan” (study plan): “plan”; subscription plan: “pakiet”
- “Free” (subscription name): “Free”
- “Get Plus”: “przejdź na Plus”
- “session” (study): “nauka” (“dzisiejsza nauka”), not “sesja”, which is the sign-in session or the exam period
- “subject” (of an exam or a plan): “przedmiot”
- “topic”: “temat”
- “area” (a weighted part of an exam or a goal): “obszar”
- “material” (the learner's own files and notes): “materiały”
- “notice” (an exam's official notice): “ogłoszenie”; an official guide: “informator”
- “position” (the post an exam is for): “stanowisko”, never “pozycja”
- “check” (an answer): “sprawdzić”, as in the “Sprawdź” button
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “przygotowywać”; learner writing: “pisać”
- “essay”: “wypracowanie”, not “esej”
- “guess” (a pick that doesn't count): “strzał” (“Dobry strzał!”, “Twój strzał: {guess}”), never “odpowiedź” or “przypuszczenie”; the verb: “zgadywać”
- “say” (aloud, in pronunciation practice): “powiedzieć”, as in “Powiedz to na głos”
- “share” (personal facts): “opowiedzieć”, not “udostępnić”
- “task” (a speaking exam's task): “zadanie”
- “hand in” (a mock or one of its parts): “oddać”
- “flag” (a mock question to come back to): “oznaczyć”
- “pattern” (a mistake that recurs): “powtarzający się błąd”
- “link” (between a cause and its effect): “związek”, not “link” or “połączenie”
- “score”, including an exam's “band”: “wynik”, not “ocena” (an essay's grade)
- Language skills “Listening” / “Speaking” / “Reading” / “Writing” / “Words”: “Słuchanie” / “Mówienie” / “Czytanie” / “Pisanie” / “Słownictwo”
- Speaking criteria “Fluency” / “Accuracy” / “Interaction” / “Range of language”: “Płynność” / “Poprawność” / “Interakcja” / “Zakres środków językowych”
- “examiner” / “another candidate” (the AI's roles in a speaking exam): “egzaminator” / “drugi zdający”
- “tap” (a beat the learner taps in a rhythm activity): “uderzenie”; the verb: “stuknij”
- “prerequisite”: “podstawy”, not “wymagania wstępne”
- “musicianship”: “podstawy muzyki”
- “study time”, the daily amount: “czas nauki”; the time of day the learner studies: “godzina nauki”
- “You've reached today's study time”: “Twój czas nauki na dziś się skończył”, in every line that starts with it
- Ending a subscription “for an important reason”: “z ważnego powodu”
- “Keep Plus” (turns a stopped renewal back on): “Zachowaj Plus”

## Plan terminology

- “Pace”: “Tempo”; the “On pace” / “Keeping pace” status: “Zgodnie z planem”
- “Finished”: “Ukończony”
- “Past date”: “Po terminie”, not “Wygasły” or “Zakończony”
- “Needs adjusting”: “Wymaga dostosowania”
- “Too tight”: “Zbyt napięty”
- “Even across skills”: “Równo we wszystkich umiejętnościach”
- “left out”: “nie zmieścić się” (“Lekcje, które się nie zmieściły”), not “pominąć”
- “Skip” (the learner's choice) and “skipped” (a test showed it's known): “pomiń” / “pominięte”
- “Daily time”: “Dzienny czas nauki”
- “rest day”: “dzień odpoczynku”

## Buddy names

## Formatting preferences

- Match the app's compact durations: “2 godz. 30 min”, “30 sek.”.
- “godz.” and “sek.” end with a period but “min” doesn't, so never end a sentence with a duration placeholder: move it earlier.
- Quote with „…”.
