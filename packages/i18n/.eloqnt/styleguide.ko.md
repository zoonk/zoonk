# `ko` styleguide

## Voice and locale

- Use Korean for South Korea and 해요체 in sentences. Instructions in sentences use “-세요”: “다시 시도해 보세요”.
- Never use “당신”. Drop the subject, or use “내” for the learner's own things in labels: “내 계획”.
- Buttons, links, menu actions and mission names use a noun or the “-기” form: “다시 시도”, “새 목표 시작하기”, “첫 레슨 끝내기”. Never “-하세요” or “-해요” on them. Choices the learner says in their own voice use 해요체: “잘 모르겠어요”.
- Lines saying what Zoonk is doing while it works use “-고 있어요” without a subject: “3페이지를 만들고 있어요…”.
- The buddy speaks in 해요체 too and calls itself “저”.
- Keep “Zoonk” and “Plus” in Latin letters. They take the particles of a vowel ending, as read “준크” and “플러스”: “Zoonk가”, “Plus를”.
- Localize illustrative exam examples for South Korea, such as 수능, 공무원 시험 and 토익.

## Placeholders

- The learner's name takes “님”: “{name}님”.
- A name or title placeholder can end in a vowel or a consonant. Rephrase so it takes a particle that fits both (“에게”, “의”, “도”, “에서”), or write both forms: “(이)가”, “을(를)”, “와(과)”.

## Glossary

- “lesson”: “레슨”, not “수업” or “강의”
- “goal” (the learner's goal): “목표”
- “plan” (study plan): “계획”; subscription plan: “요금제”
- “Free” (subscription name): “무료”
- “Get Plus”: “Plus 구독하기”
- “session” (study): “학습” (“오늘의 학습”), not “세션”, which is the sign-in session
- “subject” (of an exam or a plan): “과목”
- “topic”: “주제”
- “area” (a weighted part of an exam or a goal): “영역”
- “phase” (a part of the plan) and a “step” in a process or a flow: “단계”, not “스텝”; a solution's steps: “풀이 과정”
- “section” (a part of a mock exam): “파트”, not “영역”
- “material” (the learner's own files and notes): “자료”
- “notice” (an exam's official notice): “공고”
- “position” (the post an exam is for): “모집 분야”, never “위치”
- “check” (an answer): “확인”, as on the button; “정답 확인” where “확인” alone could read as OK
- “question”: one the learner asks: “질문”; one the learner answers, in a lesson, practice or exam: “문제”
- “write” (Zoonk generating content): “만들다”; learner writing: “쓰다”
- “guess” (a pick that doesn't count): “예상”, never “답”
- “say” (aloud, in pronunciation practice): “소리 내어 말하다”
- “share” (personal facts): “알려 주다”, not “공유하다”
- “task” (a speaking exam's task): “과제”
- “hand in” (a mock or one of its parts): “제출하다”
- “flag” (a mock question to come back to): “표시하다”
- “pattern” (a mistake that recurs): “실수 패턴”
- “link” (between a cause and its effect): “연결 고리”, not “링크”
- “score”: “점수”; an exam's “band”: “밴드”
- “memory” (what Zoonk remembers about the learner): “메모리”; the learner remembering what they studied: “기억”
- Language skills “Listening” / “Speaking” / “Reading” / “Writing” / “Words”: “듣기” / “말하기” / “읽기” / “쓰기” / “어휘”
- Speaking criteria “Fluency” / “Accuracy” / “Interaction” / “Range of language”: “유창성” / “정확성” / “상호작용” / “표현 범위”
- “examiner” / “another candidate” (the AI's roles in a speaking exam): “면접관” / “다른 응시자”
- “prerequisite”: “사전 지식”
- “musicianship”: “음악 기초”
- “study time”, the daily amount: “학습 시간”; the time of day the learner studies: “학습 시간대”
- “You've reached today's study time”: “오늘 학습 시간을 다 채웠어요”, in every line that starts with it
- Ending a subscription “for an important reason”: “부득이한 사유로”

## Feature names and states

- “Buddy”: “짝꿍”; “study buddy”: “공부 짝꿍”
- Buddy stages “Baby” / “Young” / “Adult”: “아기” / “어린이” / “어른”
- “Brain Power”: “두뇌력”
- “mission”: “미션”
- “call” (speaking practice): “대화”, not “전화”
- “Mistakes notebook”: “오답 노트”
- “Saved to your mistakes”: “오답 노트에 저장했어요”, in every line that starts with it
- “To fix” / “Fixed” (notebook tabs): “고칠 문제” / “고친 문제”
- “New” / “Learning” / “Solid” / “Mastered” (mastery): “신규” / “학습 중” / “안정” / “마스터”
- “Known before”: “이미 알던 것”; “Known” (progress legend): “아는 것”
- “Preparation”: “준비도”
- “the Trickster”: “트릭스터”
- “Broken” / “Held” (shield): “깨짐” / “버팀”
- “Phase challenge”: “단계 챌린지”
- “Weekly challenge”: “주간 챌린지”
- “Mock exam”, including speaking mocks: “모의고사”
- “Logbook”: “학습 일지”
- “Fading” (needs review): “잊혀 가는 중”
- “Overview” (course level): “개요”
- “Focus test”: “집중 진단”
- “Weekly summary”: “주간 요약”
- “Full review”: “총복습”; spaced review: “복습”
- Belts: “흰띠”, “노란띠”, “주황띠”, “초록띠”, “파란띠”, “보라띠”, “갈색띠”, “빨간띠”, “회색띠”, “검은띠”

## Plan terminology

- “Pace”: “페이스”; the “On pace” / “Keeping pace” status: “계획대로”
- “Finished”: “완료”
- “Past date”: “날짜 지남”, not “만료” or “종료”
- “Needs adjusting”: “조정 필요”
- “Too tight”: “너무 빠듯함”
- “Even across skills”: “스킬 간 균형”
- “left out”: “제외”, not “건너뜀”
- “Skip” (the learner's choice) and “skipped” (a test showed it's known): “건너뛰기” / “건너뜀”
- “Daily time”: “하루 학습 시간”
- “rest day”: “쉬는 날”

## Buddy names

- “Zu”: “Zu”
- “Noodle”: “골똘이”
- “Beep”: “삐삐”
- “Otto”: “옥토”

## Formatting preference

- Match the app's compact durations: “2시간 30분”, “30초”.
