# Player

The lesson player for Library lessons. Apps render `LessonPlayerProvider` (`@zoonk/player/lesson`) around `LessonPlayerShell` (`@zoonk/player/lesson/shell`), and load the package's translations with `playerMessages` (`@zoonk/player/messages`). The player has one look, which follows the device's light or dark theme: the full-screen task header shared with the other study screens (`@zoonk/learn/task-header`: close, the lesson's title with its minutes under it, the "…" menu and one thin progress bar), each screen starting at the top with its result right under it after a check, and one main action pinned at the bottom. A lesson the learner comes back to opens where they left off, from the run's answers. Links, images, the API client and analytics are injected by the app, and slots let it replace parts such as the completion moment or add "Report a problem" to the menu; grading, runs and completion live in `@zoonk/core/lesson-player`.

## Layout

- `src/lesson`: the player itself. One reducer (`lesson-player-reducer.ts`) drives every step kind; `lesson-player-header.tsx` and `lesson-stage.tsx` lay out the header and the screen in view, `steps/` renders each kind, `controls/` holds "Explain first", the summary sheet and the offer to ask the buddy when the learner seems stuck, `_components/lesson-pictures.tsx` shows each screen's picture (waiting on one still being drawn through the `getLessonPictures` adapter), charts and timelines come from `@zoonk/learn/visual` and tables from the screen's Markdown, `feedback/` the verdicts and the line on what happens next, `completion/` the completion moment, and `tutor/` hosts the questions sheet.
- `src/activities`: interactive activity renderers, one folder per template (see `src/activities/README.md`).
- `src/components`: the language exercise components (`exercise-step.tsx` plays alphabet, vocabulary, fill-blank, match-columns, multiple choice, reading, listening and translation screens through them) and the scene primitives they share.
- `src/questions`: the tutor, always the learner's buddy by face and name (`@zoonk/learn/tutor-identity`; a neutral "Buddy" before they pick one): its sheet, streaming, conversation recovery and Markdown, for a lesson in the player (with "Explain it more simply" and "I want to go deeper" as one-tap questions) and, through `AskTutor` (`@zoonk/player/tutor`), for a chapter, plan or finished mock on their screens. Apps import `@zoonk/player/questions/styles.css` in their stylesheet entry point, and pages that place `AskTutor` send the player's messages. Authorization, allowances, shared answers, persistence and generation stay in Core and the API.

## Styling

Shared scene primitives (`player-read-scene`, `player-choice-scene`, `step-layouts`) own layout, spacing and baseline typography; step components adapt data into them. When a screen needs a variation of a shared pattern, add a semantic variant to the primitive instead of one-off styling. Local styling fits content unique to one feature, such as vocabulary word display or grammar highlighting.
