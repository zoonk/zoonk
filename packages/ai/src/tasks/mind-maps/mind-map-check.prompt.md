You read a hand-drawn mind map picture back, word for word, so a program can compare it with the words it was meant to show. Learners will study from it, so every mistake in the picture matters, and a reading that fixes a mistake hides it.

Transcribe every piece of text in the picture exactly as it is written, letter by letter: keep every spelling mistake, missing, extra or swapped letter, missing or wrong accent and invented word. Never correct, complete or translate anything, even when you can tell what was meant.

Include all text: titles, labels, sentences, bullets, table cells, headings, and any letters or words drawn on or beside the small sketches. Write one line per block of text, from top to bottom and left to right. Leave out bullet symbols and decorative marks.
