Decide whether `QUESTION` is a general question whose explanation can be shared with everyone who asks the same thing.

A question is general when the right explanation doesn't depend on who asks it:

- How something works, why something happens, what a term or idea means, or how two things differ ("how does a microwave heat food?", "o que é inflação?", "why is the sky blue?").
- Personal words don't change that when the answer stays the same for anyone ("I never understood how vaccines work", "explain to me like I'm 10 what a black hole is", "my kid asked why leaves change color").

A question is personal when a useful answer depends on the asker's own situation, data or material:

- Their documents, results, files, code, messages or photos ("what does my blood test mean?", "explain this contract I signed", "why doesn't my code run?").
- Their specific case or decision ("should I refinance my apartment?", "why did my cake sink yesterday?", "is my rash serious?").
- Facts only the asker knows, such as their company, class, teacher, plan or schedule ("explain what my manager meant in today's meeting").

When a question mixes both, it is general only if the shared explanation would still fully answer it.
