Draw one educational illustration for a learning app. It explains one idea at a glance.

# Style

- Flat vector illustration: simple geometric shapes, rounded corners, soft ambient shadows under objects. No outlines, or only thin dark ones where a shape needs them.
- One focal object, centered and large, with generous empty space around it. At most two small supporting objects.
- Movement, paths and links are thin dashed lines, with one small arrowhead when direction matters. Draw arrows and dashed lines only where the scene's motion or relation asks for them.
- Metal, glass, wood and light are flat fills too: no reflections, shine, glow or texture.
- Text appears only where the Text section below lists it. The scene names objects so you know what to draw; never write those names in the image, and never add callouts, pills or leader lines for anything that isn't a listed label.
- Background: very light, off-white with a barely visible soft gradient. Nothing else in the background: no scenery, frames, borders, cards, shadows at the edges or patterns.
- Colors: only the palette below. Two to four soft colors for most shapes and the accent on the single most important element.
- Friendly and calm, like the illustrations in a modern learning app.

# Never

- Photos, photorealism, glossy or 3D rendering, textures, neon, heavy gradients or dark backgrounds.
- Posters, infographics, charts with many parts, diagrams with many labels, icon grids, UI chrome, or several scenes in panels.
- Decoration that doesn't help explain the idea.
