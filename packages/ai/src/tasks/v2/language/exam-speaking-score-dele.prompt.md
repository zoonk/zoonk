# EXAM: DELE (Instituto Cervantes), "Prueba de expresión e interacción orales"

Every DELE scale runs from 0 to 3 in whole bands. Band 2 is performance at the exam's level ("el equivalente a la descripción del nivel" of the CEFR), band 3 is comfortably above it, band 1 is below the level, and band 0 is far below it or the task wasn't done. The rater gives each task its bands on the analytic criteria; the interviewer gives one holistic band for the whole paper.

The criteria, by key:

- `languageUse` ("Uso de la lengua", A1 and A2): the range and control of words and structures, how the speech is linked, and how easily it's understood. At A1 band 2 the candidate speaks in word groups and short sentences, linked minimally ("y", "también"), and some listener effort is still fine; at A2 band 2 the repertoire is enough to get by in simple situations, the speech is reasonably continuous, and it's easy to understand even with an evident foreign accent.
- `taskCompletion` ("Cumplimiento de la tarea"): whether the candidate does what the task asks with the information it needs. At A1 and A2 band 2 they give the information for nearly every point on the sheet (one point missed or two answered thinly). At C1 and C2 it also covers mediation in Task 1 (summarizing the text's key points and assessing it, or using every source and following the given points at C2), the interview in Task 2 and the negotiation in Task 3.
- `coherence` ("Coherencia", B1 and B2): how ideas are organized and linked, and how the candidate keeps a conversation going. B1 band 2: short linear sequences joined with common connectors, simple conversations kept up with an occasional request for clarification. B2 band 2: clear, coherent speech with adequate though limited cohesion, a conversation kept up by working with the examiner.
- `fluency` ("Fluidez", B1 to C2): pace, pauses and pronunciation. B1 band 2: continuous and understandable with evident pauses to plan; B2 band 2: a fairly even pace with few long pauses; C1 band 2: fluent and spontaneous with only occasional loss of natural flow; C2 band 2: fluent, spontaneous and effortless. Its pronunciation part is about being clearly intelligible: at every level the scale allows an evident foreign accent, or traits of other languages that don't hurt intelligibility, at band 2 and above. It is the criterion about sound.
- `accuracy` ("Corrección"): grammatical control. B1 band 2: reasonable control of simple structures, errors that don't block understanding; B2: relatively high control, errors sometimes self-corrected; C1: consistent control, sporadic errors in complex elements; C2: consistent control even with complex elements, hardly any errors.
- `range` ("Alcance"): range of vocabulary and structures. B1 band 2: enough to get by in everyday situations, with some hesitation and circumlocution; B2: wide enough for clear descriptions and views on general topics without obvious searching for words; C1: broad, with an appropriate style; C2: very broad and used consistently and appropriately.
- `interviewer` ("Escala del entrevistador", A1 and A2) and `holistic` ("Escala holística", B1 to C2): the interviewer's overall impression of the candidate's communication across the paper: how well they do the tasks, the language they use, how they keep the interaction going and how clear they are. Rate it from this task's evidence.

`languageUse` (A1, A2) and `fluency` (B1 to C2) are the criteria about sound; at A1 and A2 `languageUse` covers more than sound, so rate its language part from the transcript as usual.
