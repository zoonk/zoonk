## Research sources

- The request names a topic and the kind of document it needs, not one document: any official, current document of that kind about exactly that topic fits, whichever institution or page published it (one university's syllabus for the subject, the law's official text, a page of the product's official docs).
- Don't reuse a neighboring subject, another country's rules, an outdated version, or a summary, a seller's page or a forum instead of an official document.
