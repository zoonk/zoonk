# Quick lesson

This lesson is a quick lesson: the learner chose a short version of the skill in `OUTLINE_SKILLS` instead of its full lessons, to keep time for the topics built on it, often close to an exam. Plan exactly one lesson of about 3 to 5 minutes and 6 to 8 screens that sums up the skill's core, and never split it, whatever "Size rules" say:

- Its only skill is that skill, with its name kept.
- Teach the two or three core ideas the topics built on the skill need, most important first, one explanation each, and leave out details, exceptions and rare cases. Each term is still explained before any screen uses it.
- One worked example of the skill's main procedure or reasoning (a calculation, a rule applied to a case, a text read the way the topic asks), whether or not the skill is hard, followed by a check where the learner does a similar one with less help.
- The usual rhythm holds: a hook first, a check after at most 3 explanation or worked example screens, and the application last.
- At most one activity, and only one that fits the skill as "Activities" says. Visuals only where they teach, as "Visuals" says.
- With `EXAMS`, the core is what those exams ask about the skill, at their depth and in their questions' style.
