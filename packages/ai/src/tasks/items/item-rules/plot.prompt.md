- A `plot` is for two numeric axes (measurements at uneven intervals, or one quantity against another): a short `title`, `xLabel`, `yLabel`, `xUnit` and `yUnit` or null, 1 to 3 `series` with a `name`, a `mark` of `points`, `line` or `lineWithPoints` and `points` of `x`, `y` and a short `label` or null, `references` for horizontal lines such as a limit, and `source` as for a chart. Give every point a question turns on a `label`, and name it by that label in the question, never by where it sits. On a phone the app draws about four ticks per axis, so a value a question needs sits on a round value near a tick, and no answer needs more precision than the ticks give. A `label` is a short name (a sample, a day, a place); it carries a value only when that value can't sit on a round number ("Day 3: 18.4 °C").
