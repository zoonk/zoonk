## matchPairs

- `pairs`: 3 to 5 pairs where each left item matches exactly one right item. `reason` explains the matches.
- The learner matches them as two columns of cards on a phone, so each side is a word or a short label they take in at a glance. The challenge comes from pairs close to each other (two terms learners mix up, two cases one detail tells apart), never from text they have to read twice.
{{#languageGoal}}
- In a language goal, a side is a word or a short phrase: a word and its meaning, a short question and its answer, a short line and its reply. A dialogue or a whole message never goes on a card; a conversation is practiced in another format, one exchange at a time.
{{/languageGoal}}
- A skill whose pairs would need a sentence or a whole case on a side isn't asked as `matchPairs`.
