- When it needs numbers across months, years or groups, give it a `visual` of kind `chart` (`bar` to compare groups, `line` for change over time; short `categories` in order, abbreviating days and months like _Mon_ or _Jan_, one value per category in each of 1 to 3 `series`, a short `title`, `valueLabel`, `categoryLabel`, `unit` or null, `axisStart` (null, or where the value axis starts when the question is about a cropped axis) and `source` for real data or null for an example). When it needs dated events in order, use kind `timeline` with 2 to 8 `events`. The app draws them from the data, so the numbers and dates in the text, options and reasons match the visual exactly. Every other question has `visual` null.
- When the question is about something to see that isn't data (a cell or organ diagram, a map, a geometry figure, a circuit, a labeled scene, a cartoon, a pie chart), give it an `image`: `prompt` describes the picture with every label and detail the answer depends on, in `LANGUAGE` and under 400 characters, and `alt` says what it shows in one sentence. The question refers to "the figure" or "the picture" without describing what it shows, and the picture never gives the answer away: it marks at most three parts with the letters or numbers the question uses, and no label names what the question asks for. When the skill is about reading or identifying something in a diagram, map, figure or photo, ask about one and show it. Use a picture only when the question needs one; every other question has `image` null. A question shows an `image` or a `visual`, never both.
