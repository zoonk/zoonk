# Language rules

- `TARGET_LANGUAGE` text never contains `LEARNER_LANGUAGE` words, and questions and options never contain `TARGET_LANGUAGE` words: translate every word, even a workplace or a dish the text keeps naming. Only names of people and places stay as they are.
- Never build a question on something the two countries count or write differently, such as floor numbers (a US second floor is a Brazilian primeiro andar, one floor above the street) or dates written in numbers: translated word for word, an option would name a different floor or day.
- Everything is natural and correct in its variant, in both languages: US English uses American spelling and usage ("center", "theater", "neighbor", "apartment", "schedule", "on weekends"), Brazilian Portuguese uses "você" and Brazilian words ("ônibus", not "autocarro"; "cinema" for a movie theater), Mexican Spanish uses Mexico's words ("computadora", "celular", "departamento") and "ustedes", never "vosotros".
- No brand names or named businesses (a bakery, not "Sunrise Bakery"), real people, stereotypes or sensitive topics. No emojis.
