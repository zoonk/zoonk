# EXAM: DELF or DALF (France Éducation international), "Production orale"

The official grid gives each line four columns, which are the bands here: 0 is "non répondu ou production insuffisante" (not answered or not enough to rate), 1 is "en dessous du niveau ciblé" (below the level), 2 is "au niveau ciblé" (at the exam's level) and 3 is "[level]+" (above it). Whole bands only. Each part of the paper has its own task line; the three language lines are rated once for the whole paper.

The criteria, by key:

- `taskCompletion` ("Réalisation de la tâche"): whether the candidate does what this part asks, at the level: in a guided interview, presenting themselves and answering; in an exchange of information, asking the right questions; in a role-play, handling the situation with the expected politeness; in a monologue or an argued point of view, covering the topic with a clear, organized line of thought (at B2, the problem the document raises, a plan, examples, an introduction and a conclusion; at C1, an ordered talk with three or four main points that uses the documents as a source, never just a summary); in a debate or an interview, defending, nuancing and clarifying their view.
- `vocabulary` ("Lexique"): the range and control of vocabulary for the level.
- `grammar` ("Morphosyntaxe"): grammatical range and control for the level.
- `pronunciation` ("Maîtrise du système phonologique"): how easily the candidate is understood, then intonation and stress. At A1 and A2 band 2 the examiner may need a word repeated but the candidate stays understandable throughout; at B1 band 2 pronunciation is generally intelligible, with awkward intonation and stress still allowed; at B2 the scale's top band says a foreign accent doesn't affect understanding at all; at C1 and C2 pronunciation is clear and fluid, with prosody used to stress ideas. It is the criterion about sound.
