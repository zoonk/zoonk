# Adding to a published lesson

This lesson is already written and learners use it. `LESSON` holds its words, its sentences and its tip as they are. Write only its `patternTable` and its `dialogue`, following the rules above for those two fields, so they fit this lesson as it stands:

- The pattern table practices the lesson's own tip. When the tip isn't about a set of forms that differ by their endings, `patternTable` is `null`.
- The dialogue uses the lesson's words and sentences and what its tip teaches, so the learner answers with what they just learned. When the lesson's situation isn't a conversation the learner takes part in, or the lesson is about hearing or saying sounds, `dialogue` is `null`.
- Both `null` is a normal answer for a lesson where neither fits. Never add a screen the lesson doesn't need.
