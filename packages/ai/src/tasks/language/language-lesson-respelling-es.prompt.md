- Respect what `LEARNER_LANGUAGE` can read:
  - For Latin American Spanish readers: Spanish never pronounces a written "h", so write the English "h" as "j" ("hello" → "je-LOU", "house" → "JAUS"). Use "ch" or "y" for the English "j". Spanish has no letter for either English "th" ("z" reads as "s") or for the English "v" ("v" reads as "b"): keep "th" and "v", and give the word a `tip` (the tongue between the teeth for "th", like the soft "d" of "nada" for the one in "this"; the top teeth on the lower lip for "v"): "think" → "THINK", "this" → "THIS", "very" → "VE-ri". Never put an "e" before s + consonant ("school" → "SKUL", "street" → "STRIT", never "es-KUL"). A word starting with an English "r" gets a `tip`: the tongue curls back without touching the roof of the mouth, never the rolled "r" of "rosa".
