# EXAM: Goethe-Zertifikat (Goethe-Institut), "Modul Sprechen"

From A2 every criterion has five bands, which are numbers here: 4 is A (the best), 3 is B, 2 is C, 1 is D and 0 is E (not ratable or not appropriate). Whole bands only, as examiners may only give these. At A1 the one criterion has three bands: 2 for the task fully done and understandable, 1 for the task only partly done because of language or content gaps, and 0 for the task not done or not understandable. The scales don't name a band for the level: the module is passed from 60% of the points, so B is comfortably there and C is the edge.

The criteria, by key:

- `taskCompletion` (Erfüllung; "Aufgabenerfüllung" at A2): content, extent and the language functions the task asks for (planning and agreeing, presenting with every slide or point covered, giving feedback and asking a question, arguing). At B1, Teil 2's band follows how many of the five slides the candidate covered well: all five A, three or four B, two (or all too briefly) C, one D.
- `interaction` (Interaktion): starting, keeping up and ending the conversation, reacting to the partner, and the right register.
- `coherence` (Kohärenz): how sentences and ideas are linked into a line of thought a listener can follow, and, from B2, fluency (a natural pace at A, slowed at B, halting in places at C).
- `questions` (Fragen/Antworten): asking and answering questions about a talk, in content and language.
- `language` (Sprache, A2): the range and control of words and structures.
- `vocabulary` (Wortschatz): the range, control and register of vocabulary.
- `grammar` (Strukturen): the range and control of structures (morphology, syntax).
- `pronunciation` (Aussprache, "Aussprache und Intonation" at C2): how easily the candidate is understood. Grade it on intelligibility alone: 4 when deviations are rare and never get in the way, 3 when deviations are noticeable but never impair understanding, 2 when they impair understanding in places, 1 when they impair it considerably, 0 when the candidate can't be understood. A perceptible or strong accent is never a reason to lower this band on its own, although the C2 scale names native-language influence: here only understanding counts. It is the criterion about sound.
