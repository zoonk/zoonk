Draw one educational illustration for a learning app. It explains one idea at a glance.

# Style

- Flat vector illustration: simple geometric shapes, rounded corners, soft ambient shadows under objects. No outlines, or only thin dark ones where a shape needs them.
- One focal object, or one small scene (one place with only the few things the scene lists, such as a pond's bank with its animals), centered and large, with generous empty space around it. At most two small supporting objects. A small scene's place is a simple flat shape (the water, the ground, a counter), never a full background.
- People are adults, unless the scene says they're children.
- The part the scene highlights is marked with the accent color, as a soft fill or a soft outline around it, never a red circle, cross or mark.
- Movement, paths and links are thin dashed lines, with one small arrowhead when direction matters. Draw arrows and dashed lines only where the scene's motion or relation asks for them.
- Metal, glass, wood and light are flat fills too: no reflections, shine, glow or texture.
- Text appears only where the Text section below lists it. The scene names objects so you know what to draw; never write those names in the image, and never add callouts, pills or leader lines for anything that isn't a listed label.
- Background: plain white. Nothing else in the background: no scenery, frames, borders, cards, shadows at the edges or patterns.
- Colors: only the palette below. Two to four soft colors for most shapes and the accent on the single most important element.
- A whole with its parts (an organ, a cycle, a map, a machine) is one clear drawing with each listed part easy to tell apart, and nothing the scene doesn't list.
- An artwork the scene names (a painting, a sculpture, a building) is drawn as a simplified version of that work in its own colors and technique, so it can be recognized and compared. Everything around it keeps this style.
- Friendly and calm, like the illustrations in a modern learning app.

# Never

- Photos, photorealism, glossy or 3D rendering, textures, neon, heavy gradients or dark backgrounds, except inside an artwork the scene names.
- Posters, infographics, charts, legends, icon grids or UI chrome.
- Two scenes in one picture: panels, halves split by a line, or two places stacked or side by side. Only the composition below draws two parts, and only when it says so.
- Decoration that doesn't help explain the idea.
