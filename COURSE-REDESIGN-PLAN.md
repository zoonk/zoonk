## Agreed product and architecture decisions

1. **One course identity, one current reusable curriculum.** Preserve Course IDs, slugs, and locale editions. Levels belong to chapters within the course; never create a Course per level. Core has overview/basic/intermediate/advanced; only overview has 3–6 chapters. Language uses explicit A1–C2. No other chapter or lesson caps. Question uses one chapter, resolving the earlier 1–3 suggestion in favor of the later definition of done.
2. **Learner path separate from content.** A course-specific plan stores intent/outcome, starting level, depth, time preference, format preferences, and selected ordered chapter IDs. Defaults provide overview, comprehensive progression from a chosen starting level, or a focused path. AI selects from actual reusable chapters for practical goals with necessary prerequisites; private goals never enter reusable content prompts. Learners can change paths later without losing historical completion. Full curriculum stays reachable. Global profile contains reusable interests, not unrelated-course level/format settings.
3. **Short guided entry.** Direct course visits and learning requests converge on a single Start action. Ask only unresolved questions, one decision per screen with understandable examples and an Other answer. Core intent distinguishes quick understanding, practical outcome, and thorough learning; level follows only when relevant. Language offers A1–C2 with everyday ability descriptions and an unsure/default route, without a compulsory test or certification claim. Question proceeds directly to its short plan. Time and format customization are optional, explained, and revisitable; skipping uses sensible defaults. Resume returning learners directly. Guest choices can be used to browse generated material and survive sign-in when generation requires an account.
4. **Calm course and lesson surfaces.** Course page has one primary Start/Continue action and the selected path/level's chapters with retained imagery. Full curriculum and edit-path actions use progressive disclosure. Avoid a dashboard of settings. Core/question/personalized teaching lessons form the primary list; quiz/practice/review are optional activities reached intentionally from a lesson or chapter and excluded from required progress and automatic continuation. Language keeps teaching activities meaningful and offers understandable course-specific format choices. Do not imply CEFR certification when a selected path is complete.
5. **Personalized discovery and ownership.** Authenticated owner-scoped request with dynamic material follow-up questions, no arbitrary question limit, and a resumable answer history. Reuse a course/path where the outcome fits; generate a private course when specific constraints require unique content. Private resolution/status/course/chapter/lesson/steps/questions/progress/generation must enforce ownership, with no shared-cache, public search, sitemap, edition, prompt, or analytics leakage. Exam remains out of scope.
6. **Tracks for multiple subjects.** Resolve independently learnable combined subjects to separate course identities and an owner-specific ordered Track. My Courses presents tracks and standalone courses calmly, avoiding repeated entries for the same course; track progress derives from course paths. Support revisiting, changing order/removing membership, partial generation failure and retry without duplicating courses or billing.
7. **Access meters generation.** Guests read existing published content anywhere but never generate. Free and paid users can choose any starting point and generate subject to atomic, retry-safe quotas. Remove chapter-one subscription gates from every consumer. A durable user/chapter grant funds the whole chapter's missing lessons and activities, including after a month reset; completed content or joining already running work consumes no grant. Claim at first owned generation of missing content, even when another learner created the chapter shells. Begin with three new chapter grants per month for free accounts, with separately centralized operational rate limits and new-outline budgets. Avoid eager child workflow generation outside this trusted boundary. Preserve existing content access after quota exhaustion. Show one understandable chapter allowance/reset/upgrade outcome, not an internal quota dashboard.
8. **Generation teaches one useful idea at a time.** Aim for 1–2 minute lessons, generally under four minutes, with a whole-lesson reading/interaction budget. Split concepts without splitting coherent ideas into repetitive fragments. Introductory/overview content uses concrete everyday language without formulas; deeper content builds intuition before formal details. Goals determine whether current AI tools, reasoning, debugging, verification, and practical judgment or academic foundations receive emphasis.
9. **Spend assets where they help.** All generated chapters retain thumbnails. All authored outline chapters receive independent thumbnail jobs, including private chapters; lesson content remains on demand. Reusable work uses strong models and one selected useful illustration per short explanation as an initial policy. Persist explicit image step indices and independently retry image jobs. Use existing diagrams/charts/tables/code visuals when appropriate. Personalized text uses luna end to end, including classification/repair paths, and images only for actual instructional value. Private context must not shape shared reusable assets. At the brief's assumed price, 194 thumbnails cost about $1.55; outline quotas must account for this expense, not only text lessons.
10. **Replace obsolete curricula without deleting learning history.** Add an explicit current curriculum version and lazily generate a replacement outline on an authenticated start when necessary. Keep the old curriculum readable until a complete validated replacement is ready; atomically install it under a version/run guard. No bulk regeneration or manual reset. Preserve LessonProgress and StepAttempt history through nullable content references plus snapshots, alongside DailyProgress/UserProgress and completion history. Carry learning credit only across exact stable known concept identity; never invent mastery through position or speculative semantic matching. Do not retain a parallel forest of obsolete curricula. Test concurrent generation, failure, retry and statistics before/after replacement.
11. **Shared capability ownership.** Core owns authenticated business behavior, persistence and orchestration; API exposes all reusable Main behavior with runtime Zod/OpenAPI parity. Apps own presentation and transport. Apple updates existing course/chapter/My Courses/account/subscription surfaces; private authoring and player are unported and must not be built here. The brief explicitly asks for editable global interests on Apple, so include it within existing account UI.
