# Presses and what a learner reads

`pnpm verify:pages --press` and `--baseline` measure what a still picture can't: what a press does to the screen, and what text a change took away. [Measuring pages](pages.md) has the rest of the run.

## Presses

A still picture can't show a press, so `--press` presses each control. On the phone, the tablet and the laptop, in a browser that draws classic scrollbars (as Windows and a Mac with a mouse do), the X3 walk presses every choice, switch, tab, disclosure, menu and dialog trigger and the sidebar's toggle, then undoes it (the old choice again, Esc, or a second press) with every write aborted, and judges each press by these lines. Links, submit buttons and other buttons are only held, with the click swallowed, since they leave the page or may write or start a model call. A press that sends a request anyway (a day chip that saves when tapped, a sheet that asks for a preview) isn't judged, since the walk aborted it and its screen is a failed save. The report's "Presses" section names what was pressed, what was only held or not judged, and why.

| Check                                 | Fails when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Press: the control moved`            | The pressed control moved or changed size by 2 px or more, such as a menu's trigger that shifts while its menu is open. A disclosure at a list's end ("2 more") that opens its rows where it stood may go straight down by exactly what opened above it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `Press: the screen moved`             | Anything before or beside the control moved, or a card, panel or control changed size; anything after it moved, unless the control opens a region in the flow and it only went straight down: a disclosure (`aria-expanded`), a tab, or a switch, checkbox or radio that reveals or hides a region under it. A control that swaps one content for another (one price for another) holds its height. A text whose own words changed ("Save" becoming "Remove focus"), or a button whose own label did, grows or shrinks around its start, middle or end, and isn't a move. For a menu, popover or dialog it reads "pushed the page instead of floating over it". A sidebar toggle reflows the screen by design, so only the toggle itself must stay put |
| `Press: the page's scroll changed`    | The press gave the page a scrollbar (narrowing everything by its width) or a sideways scroll                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `Press: not back after undoing it`    | After the undo, something isn't where it was before the press. Judged only after a press that passed, since a failed one leaves its fault behind (an accordion that closed another section). A press that took its own control away (a menu item that closes its menu), and an undo that saves (unpicking a saved choice), aren't judged here                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `Press: text over text`               | At rest or after a press, two visible texts, neither inside the other's element, cross by more than 2 px each way, line by line, each line cut to its line height and to the boxes that clip it, which a positioned panel escapes as the browser draws it. A text that shows counts even when it's hidden from screen readers, like a panel left behind another (the day's list over the buddy's intro). A menu, dialog, popover or tooltip floats over the page, and a pinned bar over what scrolls under it, so a pair counts only in one layer, or in two pinned boxes (two panels drawn over each other)                                                                                                                                           |
| `Press: reveal without aria-controls` | (look at) A switch, checkbox or radio reveals a region under it and pushes what follows, but doesn't name it in `aria-controls` with `aria-expanded`, so only its place says it's a reveal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Each failing press lists the control's role and name, the size, what moved and by how much, and a picture after it under `<page>/<size>-presses/`, with one before it when the undo brought that screen back (an accordion that closed another section doesn't), the pressed control outlined in dashed blue and what moved in red. Positions are layout positions (transforms left out, so a sheet sliding in or a pressed button's scale don't count). The undo needs no reload; the page reloads only when an undo didn't put it back.

## What a learner reads

Its report's "What a learner reads" section lists, per page, the text the base showed that the branch doesn't and the reverse, with the screens each showed on: numbers (scores, percentages, dates, counts) first, compared as numbers so one still shown in reworded text isn't listed, each with the line it was in, then controls by name, then other text. They're leads, never failures: each is removed on purpose or a fault. The base must be measured by a checkout with this section's probe, so measure another checkout's server with this one's scripts (`--checkout`).
