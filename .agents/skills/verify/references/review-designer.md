# Reviewing as a designer

Rebuild what each changed screen should do for a learner from the request, not from the builder's explanation, then judge the screens themselves. `pnpm verify:pages` measures the pages and saves their screenshots, and [zoonk-code-review](../../zoonk-code-review/SKILL.md) is the method for classifying and reporting findings.

- **Less.** For each app screen the change touches, compare its words before and after (`verify:pages` prints each page's word and text-element counts). A screen that gained words needs a reason a learner would give. For every line ask "would anyone miss it?", and say what you'd remove from each changed screen, or that nothing should go. A removal has a cost too: report a mark that told the learner something at a glance, the one sentence that said what an offer is for, or a line whose absence makes a fallback read as an error. A [marketing page](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#marketing-pages) isn't judged by its word count: say which question of a visitor's each section answers, and report a section that answers none, a claim we can't support, a second call to action and a term a stranger wouldn't know.
- **Contrast as the design system means it.** The regular look is soft on purpose: colored marks, icons and words keep their soft tones and turn strict only when a device asks for more contrast. Only neutral (gray) text is held to WCAG AA in the regular look, and that's all `verify:pages` checks there; never darken a soft color to satisfy a scan.
- **Look at everything.** Each page's `screens.png` (six sizes, light and dark) and `paints.png` (what appeared when), held to every aspect of [zoonk-design's taste bar](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#the-taste-bar). A screen a principal designer wouldn't love is a finding, with the simpler version you'd build instead. With `--baseline`, compare the sheets before and after for changes nobody meant to make.
- **Craft.** For each changed screen, [the craft read](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#the-craft-read) by an agent that didn't build it, on screenshots at 390 and 1440, beside the existing screens the PR names as doing the same thing: edges, gaps, corners, control sizes, type steps, icons, states and differences from those screens, each finding with its rule from [craft](../../zoonk-design/references/craft.md) and its numbers. A fault in a shared piece is fixed in that piece, never on the one screen.
- **Roles.** For each [role](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#find-the-piece-before-you-build-one) on the changed screens, name the piece that draws it; anything the role table rules out is a finding. Answer each role lead in the `verify:pages` report and each catalog word `verify:diff` lists ([what each check tests](pages.md#what-it-judges)).
- **What no check sees.** A reader judges these on every changed screen: a name ("your buddy", a feature's, a plan's) shown before the screen that introduces it, and "we" in onboarding and on marketing pages; a gate or a notice that shows before the learner asked for the feature, and a gated page that shows more than the gate; a sheet that holds more than its one job; a fallback that reads as an error; an offer missing the one sentence that says what it is for; and anything the user picked or ruled on that the screen no longer does.
- **The role read.** When the change draws or moves a role, touches a role's piece or was built by more than one agent, [the role read](../../zoonk-design/references/craft.md#the-role-read): one reader, every instance of each role from every area side by side, and each one that differs reported with its numbers. Screens whose own files didn't change are in it.
- **Departures.** A departure from a rule about looks is fine when the builder listed it for the user with its reason and the screen is better for it ([when a rule looks wrong](../../../guides/exceptions.md)). One that isn't listed, that makes the screen worse, or that a note only calls "kept on purpose" is a finding, unless it's in craft's [deliberate choices](../../zoonk-design/references/craft.md#deliberate-choices) or [exceptions the user granted](../../zoonk-design/references/craft.md#exceptions-the-user-granted). So is a screen that follows a rule and looks worse for it: report the rule.
- **Craft leads.** Answer every lead in the `verify:pages` report's "Craft leads" section ([what it measures](pages.md#what-it-judges)), each named by its rule's id and name in craft: fix it, or say which deliberate choice it is. Every alignment failure is fixed in its piece, and every alignment discovery lead is looked at ([alignment](pages.md#alignment)). A page that passes its measurements can still look wrong: the screenshots decide.
- **States the change reaches.** Empty, error, waiting, long Portuguese and German text, more contrast and reduced motion, checked at a phone and a laptop size. Pick them from what the change touches; `verify:pages` covers sizes and schemes, not states.
- **Keyboard and assistive tech.** Tab through the changed screens: focus is visible on everything you reach, in a sensible order, and never trapped; Escape closes what opened; names say what the screen shows (`verify:pages` runs axe and the 44 px target check). Press each key the changed controls answer, here and on a sibling screen that has the same control: it does the same thing in both, and nothing answers a key outside the kit's shortcut list ([behavior](../../zoonk-design/references/craft.md#behavior)).
- **Words and motion.** Copy fits zoonk-design's [Words](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#words) budget and follows the [shared](../../../../packages/i18n/.eloqnt/styleguide.md) and [English](../../../../packages/i18n/.eloqnt/styleguide.en.md) style guides; motion follows [zoonk-motion](../../zoonk-motion/SKILL.md) (everyday screens stay still); nothing flashes, swaps or jumps (the paints sheet and CLS sources).
- **Effort.** Fewer steps, choices and words for the same job; nothing a learner has to remember from one screen to the next. A finding that something is unclear is fixed by the [Words](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#words) fix order, never by asking for a line that explains it. The [first-glance test](../../zoonk-design/SKILL.md#the-first-glance-test) and the [persona walkthrough](../../zoonk-persona-walkthrough/SKILL.md) are where that's judged by use.
