# Characters

The buddy and the Trickster are characters, not objects. A person shows a feeling with their face and body, so a whole character hopping, popping up, landing or tipping over as one unit reads as a sticker and feels off, worse than no motion at all. Acting in code is the system: the rig in `@zoonk/ui`, with no animation runtime. It acts live at `/design/characters` (`apps/playground/src/design-system/characters.ts`), with the map of which act plays at which moment.

- **A character never moves as a single unit.** No hop, pop, settle, tip, slide or fade on the whole buddy or the whole Trickster, and none on the buddy's face in the navigation. Inside an act the body may squash, stretch or lean on its feet, as long as it reads natural, while the face and the loose parts do their own thing.
- **It acts with its parts, eyes first.** `Buddy` and `Trickster` are drawn as named parts (`data-part`: eyes, pupils, lids, brows, mouth, face, head, body, arms, antennae, the Trickster's tail, crown and sign), each turning around its own pivot; `BuddyActor` and `TricksterActor` play an act on them (`ref.play("cheer")`). The eyes lead, the brows and mouth follow, then the head and the arms, and the loose parts come last on `ease-follow`. An expression swaps the whole face while the eyes are shut or a part is squashed, and the arms never mirror each other.
- **Acts are data, timed in frames.** `BUDDY_ACTS` and `TRICKSTER_ACTS` list each part's keys in frames at 24 a second (`duration-frame`). A part starts where it's drawn and lands on the next expression's pose, so an act never jumps. Its curves are tokens: `ease-accelerate` into a pose, the ease-out for darts and actions, `ease-swing` for weight, `ease-spring` and `ease-follow` to settle. A blink is 6 frames: 2 down, 1 shut, 3 up.
- **It reacts where something happened.** Where a character appears because the learner's action did something (a mission done, today's missions, the summary, a milestone's ceremony, a challenge's intro and result), it plays the fitting act once: `reaction={{ act, delay }}` on `BuddyActor` or `TricksterActor` plays it on the moment's beat when `useCelebration` says so and draws its landing still otherwise (a reload, a revisit, Back), so the server draws the final pose and nothing flashes. In the big celebrations its whole body reacts. Where the character is the screen's subject (the buddy's chat, a duel), `ref.play(act)` reacts to each thing that happens, never on a timer.
- **It holds still where it only illustrates a screen or is small**: the sidebar and the tab bar, Today, rows and marks, pickers, empty states. `Buddy` and `Trickster` draw it still.
- **Never in the way.** Continue works from an act's first frame, and a press, ⏎ or Esc lands an act under way at once.
- **No idle on everyday screens.** After a reaction, one blink about 2 s later where its eyes are open, then rest.
- **Reduced motion draws the act's expression at once** (the face, and the Trickster's pose), and nothing moves.
- **What's around it keeps its motion.** The number counting up, the check and the badge landing, the meter filling and the burst stay as they are.
