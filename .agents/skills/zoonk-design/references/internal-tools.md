# Internal tools

Internal tools (Admin, evals, the playground, the CLI) follow [Words](../SKILL.md#words) for a staff person who knows the domain and shouldn't have to hunt. Each page answers one question, leads with what they came for and names a thing as the learner apps do. A dashboard shows its numbers as numbers, marks and charts, what needs attention first; a table is for rows to scan. A wall of unrelated sections, a sentence of mechanics under every title, raw JSON as the only view and a one-word button that destroys on the first click don't belong.
