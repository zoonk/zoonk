# The buddy

Who the buddy is, its page and its conversations, and where else the learner asks it. The page is the [app patterns](app-patterns.md#page-templates)' conversation template.

## The buddy's page

- One compact row on top: the buddy's face and name, then the day's line, then the row's actions ("Conversations" under 72rem, and the "…": change the buddy, its glasses, what it remembers, the weekly summary). The buddy's day (Energy and missions, each with its word) is that one quiet line at every width, and opens its sheet.
- Under it the open conversation, its suggestions and its composer always in reach. The buddy's words are plain text on the page; only the learner's sit in a bubble. With a mouse or trackpad the composer takes focus on open; on a phone it waits for a tap, so the keyboard doesn't cover the conversation.
- A goal has many conversations, each at its own address (`/buddy/{id}`). `/buddy` opens the one the learner asked in today, and a blank one on a new day; `/buddy/new` always opens a blank one, and the palette's Ask brings its question there. A blank one is the greeting and the suggestions: nothing is saved until the learner sends, and the address names the conversation from then on. A conversation has no title line of its own and can't be renamed or deleted.
- The list (`BuddyConversations`) is the aside from 72rem, under "Conversations": "New conversation" first, marked while the open one has no question yet, then each conversation by its first question on one line over when it was last asked (the time today, "Yesterday", then the day), the open one marked, latest first, 30 at a time with "Show more". Under 72rem the same list is a sheet behind one "Conversations" icon button before the "…": it shows once the goal has a conversation and keeps its room until then, and a row opens its conversation and closes the sheet. A guest, who can't ask, has no list and no button.
- The tab's layout draws everything around the turns (the row on top, the list, the composer) and keeps each conversation's turns, draft and answer on its way; a page draws only the turns of the one its address names. So opening another conversation, or a new one getting its address, moves nothing, and an answer still streaming is there when the learner comes back to it.

## Who it is and where the learner asks

- Every learner has a buddy: until they pick one, it's Zu with round glasses (`resolveProfileBuddy`, `picked: false`), so no screen has a "no buddy" state. Onboarding and Preferences still offer the choice while nothing is picked, and the default buddy's hello offers it too.
- **One piece draws a character** (`BuddyArt` for the learner's buddy, `Buddy`/`BuddyActor` and `Trickster` otherwise), in one of two views: the whole body, or the head alone (`view="face"`) where the body would be under about 64px. Each view's picture is measured to hold everything it can draw, so its edge never cuts the character and a change of state moves nothing; a screen picks a view and a size and never crops, offsets or negatively margins a character. A body that stands alone and has to sit in the middle of its place (an empty state, a ceremony) takes its own drawn outline (`fit="ink"`, sized by `--buddy-height`); buddies that share a row or switch in place keep the one frame. The playground's Characters page shows both, outlined.
- The buddy's day, the sheet its line opens, lists the learner's first steps, each feature to try once; on the day the last is done the list says so once more, and it's gone from the next day.
- Learners see "buddy" ("Your buddy", "your study buddy"), never "tutor", and the buddy by its name in every Ask ("Ask Zu", "Ask Zu about this subject"), both only after the screen that introduces the buddy: before it, and on every marketing page, we say "we" ([Words](../SKILL.md#words)). The buddy's tab and its sidebar row are where the name is first shown, so they keep its face and name for every learner, and the page behind them introduces the buddy in "we" words ([exceptions](craft.md#exceptions-the-user-granted)). Translations use their glossary's word for buddy and their buddy names (the `buddyNames` namespace).
- The learner asks the buddy where the question comes up, each place with its own thread: the buddy tab (the goal's conversations, where the palette's Ask opens a new one with the question waiting in the composer); a lesson screen (the dock in the aside from `lg`, a drawer quoting the question below it, with one-tap "Explain it more simply" and "I want to go deeper" on reading screens and after a miss); a chapter or a unit (Ask in its bar); a subject (a row in the aside's "Also" from 72rem, Ask in its bar below); a session or practice question (the dock in the aside from `lg`, its face after the main action below it); a mock's results (in the same places: the same icon after the main action below `lg`, "Ask {name} about this mock exam" at the aside's foot from `lg`). Opening any of them only reads the thread: nothing is generated until the learner sends, and tapping a suggestion is a send. Before a question is checked the buddy only gives hints; it never helps during a mock still running, a placement question or a timed drill's clock. A lesson screen's conversation shows only where the learner opened it, never on its own when they come back to the screen (its aside leads back to it), and a question coming back at the end of the lesson opens fresh: what was said about its earlier answer returns once it's checked. An answer's links work once it's saved; while it streams they're plain words.
- Proposed plan changes appear as a card that leads with the one change the learner would notice and its consequence, the rest behind "All changes", applied only after the learner's tap. A warning shows only when the change takes a subject out of the plan or ends it early. Every proposal, from the buddy, the exam's official guide or the plan, waits for its own answer until a newer change about the same thing (the time, the date, the focus, what the guide gives) replaces it, and then its card is marked replaced and offers no Apply. Whenever the app has a feature for the wish (a new goal with the learner's words and a matching catalog course, a mock, the chapter test, "Choose where to focus", the Mistakes notebook, a speaking call, statistics…), the buddy offers it as a card with its button instead of describing it, and never points to an outside product for it; a feature the learner's subscription doesn't include is offered like any other, and its gate shows when they start it. The features it can offer are one list in code (`GOAL_TUTOR_APP_TOOLS`), each with its card.
