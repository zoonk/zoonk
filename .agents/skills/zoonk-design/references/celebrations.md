# Celebrations

The special moments ([the family](../../../../apps/playground/src/design-system/moments.ts), live at `/design/moments`) are warmer and bolder than everyday screens and built one way: a task's result place, then the title, what was earned as readouts (the score in ink, Brain Power in violet; one head for every task's end, `MomentHeader`, and while it saves its title in place over a shimmering line, `PendingMoment`), the day's moment (`DayMoment`, under every result that earns Brain Power or Energy: missions on their violet panel, a level in its belt's colors, Energy amber, each with its 6px meter), what's next, and one pinned action that Enter presses, each part 24px from the next. A graded result (a mock's score) leads with its number, the words naming it small above. A milestone's ceremony is the loudest, full screen before the result it crowned: a centered stage, the buddy large on a disc in the new belt's color, a headline-size title. Louder means a solid fill or a tint whose color is the reward and a short motion, never a new color; each part that moves is its own element with a `data-slot`.
