# App patterns

Concrete patterns of the learning app (`packages/learn`, `apps/main`). Reuse them before inventing a new one; extend the shared piece when a screen needs more.

## Navigation

One hierarchy, three levels, each with its own bar:

- **Tabs** (Today, Journey, the buddy): the app bar. Under `lg` a fixed bottom tab bar (icon and label, safe-area padding, hidden on full-screen tasks) and a top bar with the goal switcher on the left and the learner's level (belt dot and number, opening Statistics) and avatar on the right. From `lg` one top bar with the three tabs in the middle. The active tab is selected as a whole item (icon and label on a filled pill), never only its icon. The buddy's tab wears today's missions as a ring of three arcs around its face (the buddy page's missions chip draws the same ring), and a mission done since the learner last looked fills in with a short moment.
- **Pushed pages** (a chapter, a unit, a subject, "About the exam", the mistakes notebook): `LearnPageBar` replaces the app bar. The way back on the left (a chevron named "Back to {page}"), "Ask" on the right where the page has it, and from `lg` the tabs in the middle; the page's "…" sits beside its main action, not in the bar; the bottom tab bar stays under `lg`, with the parent tab current. No goal switcher, no account. The page keeps its title in its hero; once it scrolls under the bar, the bar shows it. Back returns to the page as it was (scroll and all) when that's where the learner came from, and to the page's parent otherwise; Escape presses it.
- **Sections** (settings, statistics, the course catalog): `LearnSectionBar` replaces the app's navigation entirely: the way back on the left, one action of the section's on the right (settings: a visitor's or guest's way in, while an account logs out from the end of the settings list; the catalog: the account's menu, or a visitor's "Log in"). No tabs, no goal, no account, no bottom tab bar, no pills. A section of several pages opens on its **hub** (`/settings`: a list of the settings pages; `/stats`: the overview cards), a page with its large title; its back (and Escape) leaves the section for the app page the learner left for it (Today, or the home page for a visitor, when opened directly). Each page under the hub goes back to the hub, with the hub's name beside the chevron, and from `lg` keeps the section's pages in a sidebar beside it (each on its small tile, the current one filled; settings' sidebar opens with whose account it is and ends with "Logout"). From `lg` the hub is the sidebar too, with its first page beside it (the statistics' overview, the profile). The catalog starts its bar with the way home instead (a house: Today, or the home page for a visitor).
- Full-screen tasks and steps (lessons, challenges, mocks, the weekly summary, a goal's onboarding questions) keep `TaskFrame`'s layout and no app bar: a chevron goes back a step, an X leaves the task (in onboarding the X starts over, after asking). Onboarding shows where the learner is as segments, and each question's eyebrow names the goal and the step ("ENEM · Step 2 of 4").
- Statistics open from the account menu and the level in the top bar. Search has one entry point (the avatar menu) and ⌘K/Ctrl+K opens the command palette from anywhere in the app.
- The account menu: whose account it is (photo, name, email, and "Plus" when that's the plan), Search, then Statistics (with the level) and Explore courses, then Settings (the hub) and Help, then the way in or out. The goal switcher lists each goal on a tile in its kind's color with its time a day and days left.
- Every control in a bar (the app bar, a page's bar, a section's bar, the public pages' bar) has one height: 44px under `lg`, 40px from `lg` (`Button` sizes `bar` and `icon-bar`; the avatar too).
- What acts on one goal (pause, resume, archive) lives on that goal's page (the Journey's "…", beside "Adjust plan" in its preparation card); the goal switcher only switches and starts a new goal. A learner starts a goal ("Start a new goal"), never adds one.
- A quick explanation has no day or Journey: the goal switcher lists the ones not read to the end under "Quick explanations", each opening itself; read to the end, it's finished and leaves the switcher, and search (⌘K) finds it again.
- No logo inside the app. Logos belong to marketing pages and the login page.

## Page templates

Every page is one of these, built from the shared pieces below, so learners always see where they are, what's on the page and where its main action is.

- **Tab root and section hub** (Today, Journey, the buddy; Settings, Statistics): `Page` > `PageHeader` with a **large title naming the place** (`PageTitle`, 34px bold: "Today", "Journey", the buddy's name, "Settings"), an optional eyebrow for its context (`PageEyebrow`: today's date, the goal and its date) and one supporting line (`PageSubtitle`: the days left and the pace; the buddy's "Your study buddy · Baby"). At most one control beside the title (the buddy's "…"; the Journey's sits in its preparation card, beside "Adjust plan"). Then at most one notice, then sections.
- **Detail page** (subject, chapter, unit, exam, mistakes notebook): `DetailLayout` > `DetailAside` (`DetailHero`: a tile, `DetailEyebrow` naming what it belongs to, `DetailTitle`, `DetailFacts` as one line of facts; then `DetailActions` with the page's one main action, "Continue 32%" or "Start 0%" with the progress in the button exactly as main's course pages say it (the label, then the percentage small and muted, a chevron when there's no progress; no play icon), never a separate bar, and the page's outline "…" beside it, as tall as it: `DetailMenu`, or `ContentVoteMenu size="detail"`) and `DetailContent` with the sections. The exam's main action is "Take a mock exam". Phones read it top to bottom; from `lg` the hero and its action stay in a sticky column on the left while the sections scroll on the right.
- **Section page** (a settings page, a stat's page): under the hub; a settings page opens with its large title and description, its fields as one grouped list (each label above its value) and its actions as rows; a stat's page names itself as a small eyebrow (its icon and name) over its big number, then its chart on a surface, its numbers as tiles and how to raise it.
- **Browse** (the catalog): the large title and its one line, the category chips (sticky under the bar), then on phones one list of rows (64px picture, title, two lines of description, a chevron, no lines between rows) and from `sm` the card grid.
- **Task** (lessons, intros, mocks, results, onboarding questions): `TaskFrame`, below.
- **Conversation** (the buddy tab): the buddy's header (art, name, "Your study buddy · its stage", "…"), its Energy and today's missions as two small stat tiles, the thread under a day label with the buddy's words in soft bubbles, and suggestion chips right above the composer at the bottom of the screen on every size.

## Screen anatomy

- Widths: a single-column page is the app's 600px column; a page with two columns (the Journey with a structure, every detail page, a section page with its sidebar) is as wide as the bar's content from `lg` (`WIDE_PAGE_CLASS`, 61rem), so the page and the bar share their edges. Task screens are about 560px. The catalog grid uses the full width with as many columns of at least 14rem as fit.
- **Sections**: every block of content sits under a `PageSection` header (`PageSectionTitle`, 20px bold, with a quiet count or state on the right in `PageSectionDetail`: "7 lessons", "3 of 9"). Groups inside a section are named by `PageSectionLabel` (13px, sentence case: "Objective tests", "Physics"); sources and rules go in a `PageSectionFooter` under the content. `SectionLabel` is only for small groups inside a sheet, a step or a folded panel.
- **One surface**: grouped content sits on `Surface` (`SURFACE_CLASS`: `bg-card`, a hairline ring, `rounded-2xl`): white on a white page in light, a step lighter than the page in dark. No gray panels as containers, no inverted rows, no shadows (the catalog's cards from `sm` are the one exception, as main draws them). Notes a page wants read first use `NoticeCard`, TipKit-style: a leading tile (or the buddy's face for its suggestions), a short bold title, the message, then a small primary and ghost actions (`NoticeCardLeading`, `NoticeCardTitle`, `NoticeCardDescription`, `NoticeCardActions`); `Callout` stays for a task's one rule.
- **One list**: lists are `ListGroup` with rows (`ListRow`, `ListRowLink`, `ListRowButton`, or a native `summary`/trigger with `LIST_ROW_INTERACTIVE_CLASS`): a leading tile, mark or picture (`ListRowLeading`, `ListRowIcon`), the content (`ListRowContent` > `ListRowTitle` 15px, `ListRowDescription` 13px), a trailing count, date or pill (`ListRowTrailing`) and a chevron when it navigates. Rows have no lines between them: their spacing and leading marks set them apart, so long lists stay calm; a row without a mark of its own in a list of marked rows ("3 more chapters") takes a quiet icon in the marks' column so every title starts in one place. A line inside a card separates its parts (the session's next step, its lessons, "See today's 7 lessons"), never rows of one list. A folded group inside a list is a `Disclosure` row.
- **One status vocabulary**: `StatusMark` (a filled check once done, a filled play for the next one, an empty ring ahead) and `ChapterMark` (adds a progress ring for one begun), the same for chapters, lessons, topics and a unit's goals. Answers to pick are rows of one grouped list with the same ring at their end, filled with a check once picked (`ChoiceList`); the next lesson or chapter in a list stands out softly with "Up next" under it.
- **One main action per page, always in the same place**: on a tab root inside the hero card it acts on (Today's session card), on a detail page right under the hero (`DetailActions`, with the page's "…" beside it), on a task at the bottom on phones and under the content on `lg`. "Ask" goes in the bar of a pushed page. Secondary actions are outline or ghost buttons, never underlined links outside running text.
- A visual anchor on every page (the buddy or Trickster art, a tile, a big number, a ring); three levels of type in a block at most: the main thing, its supporting line, its meta.
- Shared kit in `packages/learn/src/_components/`: `page.tsx`, `surface.tsx`, `list-group.tsx`, `status-mark.tsx`, `detail-page.tsx`, `notice-card.tsx`, `plus-lock.tsx` (`PlusMark`, `PlusNotice`), `KindTile` and `kindTextClass` (one color and icon per kind), `FactChips`/`FactChip`, `Callout`, `StepCard` and `Steps`, `ProgressRing`, `Meter`, `Disclosure`, `DetailsDrawer`. Main's sections use `SectionFrame` (`hub`), `SectionNav` and the same `@zoonk/learn/page`, `/list` and `/surface`.
- Empty, loading and error states keep the page's header and sections' headers: an icon tile, one title, one sentence and one button where the content would be; loading never swaps the page for a different layout.

## Plus and limits

- **Nothing is hidden behind Plus.** A free learner or a guest sees every feature a Plus learner sees, in the same place and the same look, locked: its row, card, path entry or button carries `PlusMark` ("Plus" with a sparkle; screen readers hear "Available with Plus") and still opens where it would. There the feature shows as it is (a mock's lengths, a challenge's intro, the buddy's card) with the one `PlusNotice` ("Available with Plus", what Plus unlocks in one line, "See Plus" to the subscription page, which also walks a guest through creating an account).
- A feature's own home carries the notice (the exam page's mocks, the mock chooser, a mock's intro, the buddy's card); lists, timelines and Today carry only the mark, so a learner who opens the app every day isn't asked every day. At most one notice on a screen, inline, never a dialog, a toast or a repeated prompt. On a task screen "See Plus" is the main action and the notice drops its own link (`cta={false}`); inside a card that already has a surface it's `inset`.
- What the free plan had and ended (an exam plan's free first week) uses the same notice with its own title, once, where the learner meets it.
- A cap reached is not a locked feature: lessons, help and calls say until when and the one thing to do (`LessonLimitNotice`, `HelpLimitNotice`, `CallLimitNotice`: a guest creates a free account, a free learner sees Plus, Plus waits). No plan, price page or limit message shows how many minutes of calls a plan has; they say that Plus has higher call limits.

## Tasks and steps

- Full-screen tasks (intros, challenges, mocks, results) use `TaskFrame`: leave on the left, title in the middle, one main action. On phones the action sits at the bottom within thumb reach; on `lg` content and action are grouped and centered. Enter triggers the main action and Esc leaves.
- The lesson player is the exception: its action bar stays fixed at the bottom on every size, because a lesson moves through many quick steps of different heights and the main action (and Enter) must not move between them.
- Dense results become `Steps`: one card per thing to say (the score, by area, what changed, what to do next), progress dots, Continue (Enter) and Back. Skip a step with nothing to say. A result's first step is the one number or verdict that matters.
- Intros: an anchor (the Trickster or a `KindTile`), a big title, two or three fact chips, at most one callout with the rule, then the state and its action right under them.

## Structure and progress

- Every goal shows its structure in the learner's terms: an exam's notice subjects and topics in the notice's own words and order (grouped as the notice groups them), a course's modules, a language's levels and units. Each subject or module shows honest progress.
- A subject's page answers "how far am I, what's next, and is all of it covered?": "Continue 32%" under its name, its chapters as the way in (the ones done folded into the list's first row, the next one marked), and the notice's topics as the notice's own outline: its numbers or codes dropped, each item one line with its tick, folding the items numbered under it and the chapters that teach them, under the notice's own headings when it has them (ENEM's Física, Química and Biologia as section labels over their lists), with "Appears a lot" at the end of the items past exams asked most (only when it sets some apart) and where that comes from under the list. Never a second index beside the notice's numbers.
- Wide screens use the room instead of one long column where a page holds two long things: the Journey puts the goal's structure beside the path, a detail page keeps its name and main action (sticky) beside its sections. Phones keep one column in reading order.
- The details behind a number (preparation, progress) read at a glance: its parts as small rings side by side, the estimate only when there's evidence for it, and at most two things still to do.
- Time (phases, challenges, mocks, the exam day) reads against that structure as a compact timeline; it never replaces it with a long list of chapters.
- Today's lessons carry their subject, and the session says which subjects the day covers.

## Conversation

- Every chat is the learner's buddy (its face and name; a neutral "Parceiro" before one is chosen): the buddy tab, the lesson's questions sheet, the chapter and mock tutors. The lesson sheet offers one-tap asks ("Explique de um jeito mais simples", "Quero ir mais a fundo") instead of separate buttons on the lesson.
- The buddy tab is a conversation with the learner's tutor: the buddy as the anchor with its status kept compact, the conversation, and a composer that is always reachable. Proposed plan changes appear as a card that says what changes and its effect (and any warning, such as a subject left out or a plan ending early), applied only after the learner's tap. Every proposal, from the buddy, the exam's notice or the plan, waits for its own answer until a newer change about the same thing (the time, the date, the focus, what the notice says) replaces it, and then its card says so instead of offering Apply. Today shows the newest waiting one; once it's answered, "Next suggestion" brings up the next. Whenever the app has a feature for the wish (a new goal with the learner's words and a matching catalog course, a mock, the chapter test, "Escolha onde focar", the mistakes notebook, a speaking call, statistics…), the buddy offers it as a card with its button instead of describing it, and never points to an outside product for it; a feature the learner's plan doesn't include shows locked, with Plus's notice. The features it can offer are one list in code (`GOAL_TUTOR_APP_TOOLS`), each with its card.

## Lesson content

- A lesson shows what it talks about: a picture, a table, a chart or a timeline is rendered from structured content, never described in prose ("na imagem…"). A screen the learner would otherwise have to imagine always shows it, with what the app draws itself first (a table, a chart, a timeline, an activity's diagram or map) and a generated picture only for how something looks that those can't show; screens whose words are clear on their own stay text only, and no picture decorates. Generated media is made once per library step and shared; while it's being made the step says so and waits. Questions outside lessons (practice, placement, tests, challenges, mocks) show their figure the same way, drawn before the question is stored, so they never wait.
- Lessons read like stories: a reading screen leads with its portrait picture (edge to edge on a phone, as tall as the screen allows), a new screen slides in from the side the learner heads to (in place with reduced motion), and on a touch screen a swipe left reads on and a swipe right goes back wherever the arrow keys do. A swipe never answers a question, and controls, scrolling and wide tables keep their own gestures.
- A lesson gives one personal example at most, on a screen whose idea is abstract or applied, and none when the learner's life adds nothing.
- Audio is always generated in the target language; never the browser's speech engine.

## Copy

- English source strings, short and concrete; Brazilian Portuguese is the main market. No em dashes, no jargon, no promises of a pass, score or job. Follow the [copy styleguide](../../../../packages/i18n/.eloqnt/styleguide.md) and its translations' glossaries.
