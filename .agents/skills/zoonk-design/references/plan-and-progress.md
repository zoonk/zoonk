# Plan and progress

What a goal's number measures, what each plan state is called and when a challenge shows. Core decides each one and a screen only shows it, so read this before changing a screen that shows a goal's number, its state or its dates. The pages that show them are in [app patterns](app-patterns.md#structure-and-progress).

## One measure per goal

A percentage beside a goal or one of its subjects always means the goal's measure, so the overall number reads as its subjects together and finishing every lesson reads as finished:

- An exam reads **Preparation** (coverage times how well it's known, see core's `preparation-math.ts`), and each subject its own preparation by the same rule (`SyllabusSubject.preparation`). The goal's number is its subjects' together, each weighted by its share of the exam (by importance when the exam has no shares; core's `measurePreparation`), so it always sits within them, however thin the evidence. Each subject stays under 75% until its harder topics are answered and a test is taken, so the whole can pass 75% while one subject is held: the hold belongs to that subject's sheet, never to the whole as a cap, and Main shows numbers, never the stage. A subject with too few new questions answered has no right-answers number yet: its page shows a dash until then (why is behind its tap) and "6 of 8 right" after. The bar is one fill; the subjects' header says "Preparation". Lessons show only as counts ("3 of 662 lessons", "25 lessons skipped"), never as a percentage.
- Every other goal reads **Progress**: its plan's lessons studied or skipped as known (`SyllabusView.lessons`, the modules' bars added up, "6 lessons studied" under the number, the total in its sheet and what the learner already knew in the number and the bars, so a long plan never leads with a thousand lessons), each module the same (`CoverageBar`: studied, known, new, with its legend). The lessons are the ones the plan holds (core's `isInLessonScope`): what the learner's time and date fit, never the graph's full depth, so finishing the plan reads 100% and a change to the time or date changes the count, never what was studied. What the time doesn't reach belongs to the Plan's one suggestion about time, not to the number. Right answers, what's remembered and weekly challenges show how well it's known, in the number's sheet, without moving it.
- A language goal reads its **level** ("A2+ → B1+", `LevelValue`) on the Plan and Today alike.
- The Plan's numbers, its subjects, Today's aside, a subject's page and the session summary all use the goal's measure, with the same names, bars and shares.

## Plan states and challenges

- Where the plan stands is the learner against the plan's dates, never whether the plan reaches the goal, in one wording per state on every screen: "Up to date", "2 days ahead", "3 lessons behind" (in a neutral tone), "Not enough time", "Date passed", "Paused", "Finished". The word "pace" isn't shown. "Finished" means one thing everywhere (core's `isPlanFinished`): every lesson of the plan studied or skipped by a test and every phase's challenge won, so the state reads "Finished" only beside 100%, every phase done and "Continue at …", never "13 days ahead". With every lesson done and challenges left, the state is "2 challenges to go" (on Today too), the path counts only the phases whose challenge was won, and nothing offers what's next yet. A challenge shows when it comes as Today holds it (core's `getChallengeTimings`), on the path and on its page alike: "Today" ("Next time" once today is done) for each one today holds, whether Start built it or Today only plans it, and its estimated date ("~date", never earlier than tomorrow) otherwise. A phase the plan holds nothing of (a date too near for its topics) is marked "Not in your plan", stays off the roadmap and out of the phases counted done. The map reads each phase's state from the same rule as the Plan (core's `getPhaseStates`), so a finished plan has no current phase anywhere. Plans never "end": one whose date came with lessons left (to do or set aside) is "Date passed" with the lessons left, never finished, and its slot offers the one change that keeps it going, its diff shown before Apply: a goal's own date moves to the day the learner's time covers everything by; an exam's date is the exam's, so the learner is asked then, on Today and the Plan alike: "Keep studying" (every topic goes on after it, at once, and Adjust plan can turn it off) or "Archive goal" (asks first). Before the exam the option lives in the coverage sheet. Today, with nothing planned past the date, shows "Date passed" as the day's line, never a rest day; no countdown reads "The day is here" after the day.
- A challenge explains itself once, on its intro. Rows name it by what the learner does in it ("The Trickster", "Mock exam", "Call"; never "Phase challenge" beside it) and show when it comes. A loss offers the "Rematch" on its result, where it happens. Until the learner has taken one of the plan's challenges, the plan moves phase ends on its own instead of asking about challenges they've never seen. A phase whose lessons a test found known has no challenge (nothing is won or earned), nor does a phase with no lessons in the plan. A phase that keeps lessons but whose challenge has no room before the plan's date has it left off the calendar, never pulled to an earlier day or piled onto the last: a day runs past the learner's time by one challenge at most. The Plan marks that challenge's row "Not in your plan", with why behind the mark, the phase and the plan aren't done without it (once every lesson is done, the state is "Doesn't fit", since winning the rest wouldn't finish it), and it comes back on its own with another study day before the date or a later date; more minutes on the same days may not make room.
