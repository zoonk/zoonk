# Journeys and unhappy paths

For each surface the change reaches, walk the main path and the unhappy paths that apply. Pick by risk; you don't need every line every time.

## Onboarding and goals

- A vague goal, a typo, a goal in another language, an exam the Library doesn't know.
- Changes an answer, goes back, quits halfway and returns later, on another device.
- No date, a date too close for the content, "Study everything", bases set to skip.
- The goal understanding or the plan takes long: what does the screen say while waiting?
- A guest signs up in the middle: nothing typed is lost.

## Today and sessions

- Day one, then the next morning, then after missing three days.
- Leaves mid-session and comes back; studies on the phone after starting on the laptop.
- Lessons still being written; everything done early; a 5-minute day.
- A device in a different time zone from the goal's.

## Lessons and practice

- Wrong answers, a lucky guess, asks the tutor, leaves mid-lesson and resumes.
- Long texts, math, tables, pictures, audio; a small phone; keyboard only.
- AI answer slow or failing: is the learner told and able to continue?

## Plan changes and the buddy

- Focus on an area, "less X", add a topic, change the date or daily minutes, skip basics.
- Declines a proposal, undoes a change, asks something ambiguous.
- The card says exactly what changed and nothing more; the plan shows the same thing afterwards.

## Exams and mocks

- Free learner sees mocks locked; Plus learner takes a full mock with its real clock, sections and written part.
- Interrupted mid-mock, retakes, waits for the essay grade, reads progress over several mocks.
- The day before the exam, the exam day, after the exam.

## Progress, account and limits

- Progress with little evidence reads honestly and never promises a result.
- Hits a free limit; upgrades; cancels; switches language or theme; deletes the account.

## Anywhere

- Back button, refresh, double tap, a network error, an expired session.
- Empty, loading, disabled and error states have a clear next step.
- Copy is short, concrete and kind, with no jargon the learner didn't use first.
