# Persona catalog

Pick personas whose differences matter for the change. A persona is a person with a goal, a life and a history, not a test account, so walk as them: in their language, with their time, device and patience, knowing what they know and nothing more. No persona has read the code or a spec. Each knows only the product words that screens they've already seen showed them (the "Doesn't know" column), and on day one that is none: not plan, focus, phase, Brain Power or mock exam. A word they don't know is a finding, not something to explain to them.

When an issue or feature has no persona that fits it well, add one here with the same fields, so the next walk can reuse it.

## Seeded personas (no AI cost)

The seed writes these learners on every reset, at a realistic point of their plan: their history and this week are written by hand, and the rest of each plan is the one the planner makes from them. On the dev server, sign in with the account's email and the newest code for it in the dev mailbox; E2E builds also take the seed password (`packages/e2e/src/fixtures/personas.ts`). E2E tests use private copies through `asPersona` (`apps/main/e2e/learn-personas.ts`), so they can change the plan without touching the shared ones. For a walk that changes anything, use your own copy from the [persona command](passes.md#persona-command). Writing the copies costs nothing, and opening their pages starts no AI. Changing a plan or opening a session block is a real learner's request, though: on a fresh dev database most of Ana's ENEM topics have no lessons yet, so her first plan changes write the coming weeks' lessons with real AI (about $4.30 the first time). Those lessons stay in the shared Library, so later copies on the same days reuse them at no cost.

### A guest

A guest isn't a seeded account you can sign in as: a guest has no email sign-in, and guests inactive for 30 days are deleted. Make a fresh one instead, the way the app does. In a private window, a visitor becomes a guest on their first step that needs a session (answering a public lesson's first question, sending a goal on `/start`); to start as one, send `POST /api/auth/sign-in/anonymous` with an `Origin` header of the app's URL from the browser's context, as E2E's `ensureGuest` does (`packages/e2e/src/fixtures/guests.ts`). Walk them as the persona who came from a public lesson, finished it and typed a goal without signing up. Watch for the sign-up moment, nothing lost on sign-up, and guest limits. A public lesson costs nothing; a goal the catalog doesn't cover writes its plan with real AI, as anyone's does.

## Lenses to add to any persona

Give one persona per walk at least one of these: a 390 px phone; a screen reader or keyboard only; larger text and reduced motion; a learner whose language isn't Portuguese, when the change has copy or translations; a device in another time zone; a slow network or a slow AI answer; a week away; a mind change halfway through (new date, fewer minutes, another focus).
