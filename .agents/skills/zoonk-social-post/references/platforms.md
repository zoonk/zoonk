# Platforms

What each platform needs, with where each claim comes from and when it was checked. Studies here are observational: they show what went with more reach or engagement in their sample, not what causes it. Gathered 19–20 November 2026. Re-check a claim before it decides something, and refresh this file when it's six months old or a platform announces a ranking or format change.

## Every platform

- **The first line carries the post.** No platform's cutoff before "more" was verified on current phones, so write as if only the first line shows.
- **Sizes:** stills at 1600×900 (X, LinkedIn) and 1080×1350 (Instagram, LinkedIn); clips at 1920×1080 (X, LinkedIn), 1080×1350 (Instagram's feed, LinkedIn) and 1080×1920 (Reels), 60 fps, silent. Current uncropped feed dimensions weren't verified for any platform; look at the post in the composer's preview before publishing.
- **A founder's account outruns the brand's:** on LinkedIn, personal profiles led company pages on impressions and interactions at most account sizes ([Metricool, 2026](https://metricool.com/wp-content/uploads/Linkedin-Study-2026-EN.pdf)); on X, 10 of the 12 recent launch posts above 50K views in the owner's For You feed came from founders and team members, not companies (feed capture, 19 November 2026). Post the lead from the owner's account and let Zoonk's account follow.

## X

- **Short wins:** in the owner's For You feed, the 16 complete posts above 50K views had a median of 81 characters (100 across all 95 organic posts), and none used hashtags (feed capture, 19 November 2026).
- **Launches carry a product video:** 11 of the 12 recent launch posts above 50K views had one (same capture). The two company launches opened with "Introducing" and the update's name.
- **The link goes in the post.** X's head of product said link posts lost reach because the in-app browser covered the post, not because of a penalty, and described a fix being tested ([Axios, 21 October 2025](https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-media-trends-a6e42ba0-ae85-11f0-87d6-3dbac628b68f)). Link posts did have lower engagement in a 2025 sample ([Buffer, 14 October 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/links-on-x/)), so measure clicks as well as views.
- **Formats:** in Buffer's 18.8 million posts, text had the highest engagement rate, then video, then images, for regular and Premium accounts alike ([Buffer, 14 October 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/links-on-x/)). A thread of replies, one feature each, keeps the lead post short.

## LinkedIn

- **First person in the first line:** posts that opened in the first person had 19% more reactions in 116,000 posts compared against each author's own ([Flypost, 17 October 2026](https://www.flypost.io/learn/linkedin-algorithm-study-data)). No length was better than another.
- **No bait:** LinkedIn's 2026 update targets engagement bait, pods and automated comments; posts asking for a keyword in the comments had 24% fewer reactions after late July 2026 (same source).
- **Hashtags:** one or two went with no change; three or more with 7% fewer reactions (same source). Use none.
- **Links:** link posts had about 13% fewer reactions (same source), but the click is the point of a launch, so the link stays in the post.
- **Formats on a company page:** documents (a carousel uploaded as a PDF) averaged 1,234 impressions, against 953 for a single image and 668 for a video; polls led, but a poll is bait for a launch ([Metricool, 2026](https://metricool.com/wp-content/uploads/Linkedin-Study-2026-EN.pdf)).

## Instagram

- **Carousels get saved, Reels get reached:** in 24 million posts, carousels had nine times the saves of single images and Reels four times their interactions ([Metricool, 16 June 2026](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)). A launch pairs a carousel of stills with a Reel of the demo.
- **The second slide stands alone:** Instagram often shows a carousel again starting at the next slide to someone who scrolled past ([Instagram's head, as reported by ALXO, 24 September 2026](https://alxo.ae/whats-on/instagram-ranking-2026-signals-on-the-record/)). Slide two must make sense to someone who never saw slide one.
- **The caption's first line is the whole idea**, since the feed shows little more.
- **Hashtags:** posts with hashtags had about a third fewer views and interactions than average ([Metricool, 16 June 2026](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)), and Instagram reportedly limited posts to five ([Cinco Días, 19 December 2025](https://cincodias.elpais.com/smartlife/lifestyle/2025-12-19/instagram-va-a-limitar-el-uso-de-hashtags-para-evitar-el-spam-en-las-publicaciones.html)). Three specific ones at most, or none.
- **Links don't click in captions:** write "zoonk.com (link in bio)" and keep the bio link current.
- **Original images only:** accounts posting mostly reposted photos or carousels stop being recommended to non-followers ([Digital Camera World, 1 May 2026](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/two-years-later-instagram-is-finally-giving-photographers-the-same-protection-as-videographers-with-this-key-change)). The studio's stills are original by construction.
- **Reels:** 1080×1920, brand and promise in the first seconds, text inside the safe box ([zoonk-video's formats](../../zoonk-video/references/formats.md)).
