# Pick the model

Which model and reasoning effort a task gets. Always use the latest version of each model, resolved from the live catalog: write "the latest Opus", never a version number, in these instructions and in a brief. Map effort names to each provider's supported equivalents.

Claude and OpenAI models pair by tier: Haiku and Luna, Sonnet and Terra, Opus and Sol, Fable and Astra. Where a row names both families, pick the one whose provider comes first by reset ([providers](providers.md)).

| Task                                                                           | Model and reasoning                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Design: UI, UX, layouts, interactions, motion                                  | Opus at high; Fable at high for a major new feature or a big redesign                                                                                |
| Writing: copy, docs, marketing, prompts                                        | Opus at medium; Fable at medium when Opus's quality falls short; Terra at medium when neither is available                                           |
| Everyday engineering and debugging, including multi-PR feature work            | Opus or Sol at high                                                                                                                                  |
| Well-scoped coding: bug fixes, feature iteration, spec-driven work             | Opus or Sol at high                                                                                                                                  |
| Major refactors, migrations, architecture planning                             | Fable or Astra at high                                                                                                                               |
| Code review                                                                    | The other family's top model at high: Astra reviews a Claude model's work, Fable reviews a GPT model's ([cross-model review](cross-model-review.md)) |
| Investigating a problem, filing an issue or a feature request                  | Opus at medium                                                                                                                                       |
| Computer use: driving a browser or device, persona walks                       | Terra at medium                                                                                                                                      |
| Finding and pulling out text: searching code, reading logs, collecting numbers | Haiku or Luna at medium                                                                                                                              |
| Summarizing or explaining what several files or logs say                       | Sonnet or Terra at medium                                                                                                                            |
| Classification, routing, tagging, high-volume extraction                       | Haiku or Luna at medium                                                                                                                              |
| Unsure                                                                         | Opus at medium                                                                                                                                       |

- **Claude for design and writing:** design and writing stay on Claude models (Fable or Opus) even when an OpenAI provider has more room. Design is whatever decides or judges what a learner sees: layout, components, spacing, motion, a native port of a screen, the first-glance, craft and role reads, and the read of a PR's pictures. Writing is every word a person reads: copy, docs, these instructions and skills, prompts, commit and PR prose. A GPT model still takes the code around them (core rules, checks, lints, tests) and its side of a [design debate](design-debate.md), which is read-only. If no Claude model is available, use Sol at the same effort for design and Terra at medium for writing, track the pending work, and have Fable review it at high as soon as Claude is back.
- **An outside reader for a large copy change:** when a change adds or rewords many strings, a Gemini model (Neon at high) may read them as a copywriter, given the strings with their translations and the style guides. It only reads: a Claude agent checks each finding against its screen, writes the final wording and applies it, because a reader that sees strings without their screens gets some wrong and its rewrites don't follow our style.
- **Fable and Astra fallback:** when a family's top model isn't available, use the next one down at one effort level higher: Fable at medium becomes Opus at high, Fable at high becomes Opus at xhigh, and Astra falls back to Sol the same way. Work at xhigh never falls back to max: Fable at xhigh becomes Astra at xhigh for architecture and Opus at xhigh for a redesign, and Astra at xhigh becomes Fable at xhigh.
- **No low or max:** use low or max reasoning only when the user asks for it. Low misses too much for what it saves, and max costs far more than xhigh for little gain. Past high, the next model up at a lower effort usually beats one more effort level.
- **Check medium coding and design:** coding or design work done at medium reasoning gets checked by another model before it counts. For code, the [cross-model review](cross-model-review.md) is that check. Other medium work (investigations, issues, computer use, searching, summarizing) needs no extra check, since the lead reads what it reports.
- **Escalate in order:** when a model's work falls short, first give it a way to check itself (a test, a build, a script that calls the endpoint), then raise its effort one level, and only then move to the next model up, at a lower effort than the one that fell short.
