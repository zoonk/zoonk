# Web links and prefetching

How links to app pages prefetch, and what a tap shows while its page is on the way.

- Links to app pages prefetch in full (`LearnLink` and `ListRowLink` do by default; main's `Link` takes `prefetch`), so pages whose content depends on their URL paint at once. Opt out only for a destination that does something when opened (`prefetch={false}` for practice, which picks a fresh set; `prefetch="auto"` for content that may still be being written, such as a lesson or the Library course a building plan offers). A tap shows at once even when its page isn't there yet: tabs, sidebar links and list rows give way while pressed (`press`), and a tab whose page is still on its way after 400 ms takes the current tab's look (`useLinkStatus` in main's `LearningLink`; a prefetched page renders before then). A plain link that loads another document in the tab, such as sign-in on the API, is a `PendingLink` (`@zoonk/ui/patterns/links/pending`): the browser keeps the old page up until the new one answers, and its head script marks it busy even before hydration. A list of links without bound prefetches on intent (`prefetch="intent"` on `LearnLink` and `ListRowLink`: the page's shell as a row scrolls into view, and the whole page as soon as the row is hovered, focused with the keyboard or touched).
- Every page's App Shell is static, so a default `Link` into a private page prefetches only that shell ([Main](../../apps/main/AGENTS.md)).
